# Slime Civ (game/)

Plain JavaScript ES modules, zero dependencies. Design: `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`; build plan: `notes/impl/BUILD_PLAN.md`.

## Run
- `./run` builds `slime-civ.html` and serves it at http://localhost:5714/slime-civ.html (no-cache).
- Or `node tools/build.js` and double-click `slime-civ.html` (self-contained, works over file://).
- `?seed=N` in the URL picks the world seed.

## Possession (S4b)
- **Q** (or the Possess button in the villager panel) guides one adult; Esc, Q or the banner button lets go. One villager at a time. It auto-releases only on collapse (health under 10% of max; death ends it too), never on need warnings.
- **While guided:** `WASD`/arrows step one cell at a time (hold to keep walking; cardinal moves; up/down one level like the pathfinder), `E` or **left-click** uses what is next to you (facing first): tree = chop, berry bush = pick, grass = cut, loose stack = lift, planned piece = take from the stockpile if you stand in it, deliver, finish, rock you face = dig. `C` eat, `V` drink (beside water), `N` sleep (on a bed beside you, else the ground), `G` put down what you carry (in the stockpile). Needs drain as usual; nothing relieves them but you (a toast warns when one turns critical). The camera follows at about 3x the god-view zoom, game speed is capped at 2x and fast-forward is off, and a "POSSESSING <name>" banner shows needs, what they are doing, the last refusal ("too far - walk next to it first") and a hint line. The Why panel says "You are guiding <name>".
- **Same pipeline as the AI** (`sim/possess.js`): `possess {id}`, `release`, `pinput {id, dx, dy, tid, verb, x, y, z}` go through `sim.apply` (command log; replay and saves hold, `tests/possess.js`). A pinput sets the villager's latest intent (the direction persists until changed, so holding a key is one command); `controller.control` hands a possessed villager to `playerStep`, which makes the same `Move` step or `Task` verb the job board makes. `tests/possess.js` proves an AI villager and a possessed one walk the same cells and chop for the same number of ticks, and with `INSPIRE.speed = 0` are tick-identical.
- **Divine inspiration** (`sim/inspire.js`, one place): +10% work speed (not walking), +15% skill XP, 600-tick grace for the reservations the villager held before possession (they are then given back to the village). **Steadfast** is `steadfast(v)` in `sim/possess.js`: there is no fear / unsafe-avoidance system yet, so it is a documented seam, not behaviour.
- **Bot** `tests/bots/possess.js`: a scripted player (walks with pinput, presses interact) possesses the best chopper for one season, alone in the world, vs the same villager with a chop order: output ratio 1.10-1.20 on 5 seeds, band is <= 1.25.

## Gameplay fixes (S4b)
- **Keep-N no longer starves the village.** Non-food keep-N jobs (chop for logs/sticks, gather grass) use at most 70% of the adults, and at most 20% while food, beds or racks are short (`keepFull` in `sim/jobs.js`). The last 25% of stockpile cells open only to food (`FOOD_RESERVE` in `sim/items.js`; non-food may only top up its own stacks), loose non-food that cannot be stored is not hauled, a villager stuck with such a load puts it down (`dump`, village-critical class because full hands block foraging), and keep-N idles when there is no room. `tests/bots/keep999.js`: keep 999 logs from day 1, 2 years, 10 seeds.
- **Winter forage** (§11.17): in winter a forage job digs roots at a grass tuft (`root` verb: 240 ticks of work, 3 rations, the patch recovers in 4 days; stored as the usual food item). About a third of a summer forager-day.
- **Deconstruct** tool in build mode: drag over built walls, floors, roofs, doors, beds, racks; each becomes a marked piece (`dsite`), taken down by villagers as a standing-class chore (shares the 70% hand cap), ~75% of the materials come back loose, F.PLAYER cells only, no XP. Erase also cancels marks.
- **Almost a room**: click a walled and roofed space that has no door yet to see "Not a room yet - needs a door to close" (with the gap outlined), or "door planned" when a door ghost is already there (`sim/proom.js`, pure).

## Building (S4a)
- **Plan, then villagers build.** Ghosts are `csite` entities; villagers take materials from the stockpile, deliver them, and finish pieces as derived STANDING-class jobs (never an order), at most 70% of the adults at once. Food emergencies and personal needs still win. Supports-first: floors, walls, then roofs (need a wall within 3 cells or a neighbouring roof), the door last. Erasing a ghost drops what was delivered as loose items.
- **Cells and materials** (append-only ids in `sim/world.js`): wattle wall (2 sticks, insulation 0.4), log wall (1 log, 0.5), dry-stone wall (2 stone, 0.6), packed-dirt floor (1 dirt), thatch roof (1 grass, 0.5), log roof (1 log, 0.5); door 3 sticks. Stone and dirt come only from the **dig** order (right-click rock, or the Dig button/tool; a 5x5 patch; no XP). Doors, beds and racks are furniture entities.
- **Level and roofs.** Build coordinates are the STAND cell: a wall fills it, a floor is the cell below, a roof is an ordinary solid cell one above (`z+1`). Rooms ask "is something solid within 3 above me?", so natural overhead rock counts too and pathing/rendering need nothing special. The level you view is the level you build on.
- **Rooms** (`sim/rooms.js`): flood fill of stand cells closed by solid cells or doors, up to 128 cells (bigger = yard). Enclosed = closed and fully roofed. Warmth bonus = 0.75 x mean insulation of walls, doors and roof (wattle+thatch+door about +0.34), plus a small huddle bonus per other occupant; the Warmth overlay (T) shades rooms and prints the bonus. Villagers choose a bed (or floor) in a warm room when the air is below 0.55. Purposes (`shelter`, `workshop`) are zones the player assigns; checklist and graded quality are in `sim/roomeval.js`.
- **Blueprints**: Capture a rectangle of built pieces, rotate, stamp x N (each copy is one `build` command), swap wall/roof material at stamp time, flag **auto-build** so the village starts a copy itself when it is short of beds. Saved in the game save and, with the Library button, in the browser.
- **Sim commands added**: `build {cells:[{w,m,x,y,z}], bp?}`, `erase {x0,y0,x1,y1,z}`, `purpose {x,y,z,p}`, `blueprint {op: capture|add|remove|auto|rename, ...}`, and the `dig` order verb (`order {verb:"dig", x, y, z}`, z = level of the solid cell). Actions/tasks used: `take`, `deliver`, `construct`, `dig` (all Task verbs, same runner as every other job).
- **Why preview**: `plan(sim, v, false, null, true)` is a pure dry run; while paused after a command the Why panel shows "next:" from it.

## Skills & genetics (S5)
- **Skills** (`sim/skills.js`): dig, build, craft. `level = settle(potential * (1 - e^(-xp/K)))`, K = 40 (D21); `effect = 1 + 0.8 * level`; the K6 rested curve (full XP to 60% of a 1200-tick working day, floor 50%); challenge-matched gain. Gathering, foraging and chopping are skill-less (flat base speed + the speed/strength trait). Beds, racks and sun-drying are craft; walls, floors, roofs, doors are build; the dig verb is dig.
- **Seam for S6/S7:** every XP award is `gain(sim, v, skill, value, isNovel, difficulty)`. A mining verb calls `gain(sim, v, "dig", value, novelCell(sim, x, y, z), 0.8)` (hard rock); a kiln/smelt verb calls it with "craft" and difficulty 0.7/0.9. A verb declares `skill`, `tr` (the one trait that scales its speed, clamped to +-25%) and optionally `base`; a step may override the skill with `sk`. New work that should split villagers needs its own job kind at the same class, like `build_site` / `craft_site`.
- **Anti-grind:** `F.USED` (the "virgin" bit, never cleared) plus `F.PLAYER`; `novelCell` is the test. tests/bots/grind.js covers place/dig, build/take down/rebuild, repeat racks.
- **Profession** = label from the top skill (>= 0.2): digger / builder / crafter, else forager. The preferred craft (top skill) gets +10 in the job score; skill fit is 60 x level (0.7 -> 42 against <= 20 path cost). Foraging, gathering and sun-drying keep a flat fit of 15.
- **Genetics** (`sim/villager.js`): nine traits (strength, speed, stamina, intelligence, drive, leisure, fertility, longevity, hardiness). Child = mixture inheritance + 5% noise + 4% sports (+-8-15%) + `settle()`. Convex upkeep: strength on food, speed/stamina/intelligence on energy, drive and leisure on mood sag. Genes move outputs at most +-25% (`geneMul`). Solo split (`{type:"split", id}`): once per life, clone regresses 25% to the village mean with stacking load.
- **Watching and Teach:** within 4 cells of someone 0.15 better at the same craft: +15% XP. `{type:"teach", id, pupil}` (inspector button when two villagers are selected): while both work the same craft within 3 cells the pupil gains x3 and the teacher works at x0.8. Give both the same order so they end up side by side; no automatic pairing yet.
- **UI:** inspector shows skills with a profession label, nine traits with tooltips, a lineage line, Teach and Split buttons; the Why panel explains skill fit and preferred-craft points in words.
- **Saves:** version 4; v2/v3 saves migrate (gather/chop XP dropped, build kept, new traits at 1).
- **Known gap found in S5 (fixed in S6, see Sleep commitment below):** an order beat a half-slept need, so a digger far from his bed was pulled off sleep, walked to the work, tired and walked back (dig was ~1 cell/day in tests/bots/k6.js).

## Geology & mining (S6)
- **Strata** (`sim/worldgen.js`, `sim/geology.js`; GDD §11.5): grass or sand on top, 3-4 cells of soil, 5 cells of **soft rock** (`M.SOFT`, yields stone), then **hard rock** (`M.STONE`, slower, difficulty 0.8) to the bottom. The soft rock holds **flint nodules** (`M.FLINT`, about 2.4% of cells) and **malachite** (`M.MALACHITE`): three veins of 40-60 connected cells, two of them "stains" (a seam of malachite runs from the vein up through the soil to one green cell on the surface, so "follow the green stain" works) and one exposed in the far wall of the cave. A **clay bank** (`M.CLAY`) lies on both banks of the stream at two spots. **One cave** (a ramp cut into a hillside down to a hall about 10 x 8, 20-35 cells from camp). **Two ridge cells** (local height maxima, `F.RIDGE` on the surface cell). **Tubers** (`tuber` plants, 3 rations, any season) join the winter-root forage. The surface, camp, berries, trees and tufts are exactly what S5 had (the geology is laid on afterwards).
- **Validator** (§11.10 slice subset, `validate` in `sim/geology.js`, run by `generateWorld(seed)`): clay, flint, malachite, wood and a ridge each need >= 2 separate sources within 64 cells of the camp that a villager can walk to (or dig down to), and the cave must be reachable. If not, the world is regenerated from a derived seed (`mix32(seed ^ 0x5eed5eed, attempt)`, up to 8 attempts) and `world.gen = {seed, attempts, ok, fails, counts}` records it. 4 of seeds 1-20 re-roll once. `tests/geology.js` checks 20 seeds (worldgen only).
- **Data on `sim.world.geo`** (derived from the seed, never saved): `hs` surface height per column, `flint` [idx], `malachite` [{kind, cells, tell}], `clay` [{cells}], `caves` [{id, mouth, cells, floor, cx, cy, zf}], `ridge` [{x,y,z}].
- **Dig table** (`sim/dig.js`): per material, the time, the item and how many. Dirt/grass 1 dirt, clay 2 clay (140 ticks), soft rock 1 stone (200), flint 2 flint (240), malachite 2 malachite (240), hard rock 1 stone (320, hard). XP goes through `gain(..., novelCell(...), difficulty)` (0.8 for hard rock) as before. Items drop loose at the hole and are hauled to the stockpile by the baseline hauling. Items added: tuber, flint, clay, malachite (stock bars, survey, inspector handle them; tubers count as rations).
- **Area mining** (`sim/mine.js`; command `{type:"mine", op, ...}` through `sim.apply`; a rejection leaves a blocker enum in `sim.lastBlocker`):
  - `op:"region"` `x0,y0,x1,y1, z (level of the top layer of solid cells), layers`: a box, each layer below the top inset one cell on the x0 side so it is stepped like a stair and villagers can climb out (D22). Plants on marked cells are uprooted.
  - `op:"depth"` `x,y,z (the stand level), levels, dir?`: a straight stair of `levels` columns (2 cells dug per level, the stand cell and its headroom), first direction of E/W/S/N that stays in rock.
  - `op:"vein"` `x,y,z`: a **seen** ore or clay cell; digs every connected seen cell of that material (26-neighbourhood), growing as digging shows more.
  - `op:"seek"` `item (flint|malachite|clay), x?,y?`: digs toward the nearest **seen** source of the item. If none has been seen it tunnels a square spiral (rows 3 apart, out to 24 cells) around the anchor (default the camp) at soft-rock depth (5 below the surface stand) until one is sighted, then goes for it and follows the vein until 6 units are out. `{op:"cancel", id?}` cancels.
  - Designations live in `sim.mine` (saved, hashed). `mineTick` (every 20 ticks) rebuilds each one's queue `q` (cells to dig next); `jobs.js genMine` reads it and offers **one derived standing-class job `mine`** (skill dig, never inside a dry-run). A cell whose neighbours are walkable only in a sealed pocket is not queued: a flood fill from the camp (`reachMap`) decides, and a Dijkstra tunnel planner (`tunnelPlan`: open cells free, each dug cell 10, hard rock +5) plans the stair/tunnel to reach anything else. Hand cap: 70% of adults, but only 20% while the village wants food, beds or racks (as keep-N).
- **Fog of knowledge** (`sim/seen.js`, GDD §13.8): `sim.seen.b` has one byte per cell. Known from the start: every column from its top cell up, plus side faces open to the air. A villager marks the 3x3x3 block around the cell they stand in whenever they move, and underground (rock overhead) also 24 short rays (3 cells) at their level, stopped by the first solid cell; a dug cell shows its neighbours. Only the log of cells seen after generation is saved (run-length pairs; `v.lk` is saved on each villager). `sim.seen.known[mat]` lists seen ore and clay cells, which is all seek-X is allowed to ask. `isFrontier(sim,x,y,z)` = an unseen cell next to a seen one.
- **Renderer**: unseen cells draw as dark unknown; open space under rock (tunnels, the cave) is tinted; ore has flecks (malachite green, flint dark blue, clay terracotta); marked cells get a purple wash (brighter = next in the queue). A z-slice that cuts through a hill shows the cut as dark unknown until dug into: use `[` `]` or the minus/plus in the build bar to follow a mine down.
- **UI**: build mode > Dig has Region (drag; "levels down" box), Stair down (click; "levels"), Follow vein (click an uncovered ore cell), Seek (flint/malachite/clay, then click where to start), Order selected (the old 5x5 patch) and Cancel digs. Clicking any bare cell opens a cell card (`cellInfo` in `ui/text.js`). Help text and `BLOCKER_TEXT` cover the new rejections (`NOT_ORE`, `NO_ROUTE`, `NOTHING_TO_DIG`, `TOO_BIG`, `UNKNOWN_ITEM`) and the `mine` job.
- **Sleep commitment** (`genNeeds` in `sim/jobs.js`): once a villager starts to sleep (or walks to bed to), their sleep need is held at u = 0.9 (score 360, above any order's 345) until energy reaches `SLEEP_DONE` = 0.75, so an order no longer drags a half-slept villager away. Hunger, thirst and cold above 0.9 and village-critical food still win. `tests/mining.js` fails if the line is reverted.
- **Saves**: version 5 (new strata make v4 saves load with their deltas onto the new world; fog and designations start empty).
- **Tests**: `tests/geology.js` (20 seeds, worldgen only), `tests/mining.js` (fixture-based: region, stair + walking down, vein, seek with the face in sight, seek through fog, fog, sleep commitment, hard rock, anti-grind, save round-trip with the seen log, determinism). Fixtures `mine` (a stair of 6 levels and a 6-cell tunnel east of camp in soft rock, an exposed malachite face, and a hidden pocket 3 cells behind it) and `ores` (12 flint, 12 clay, 12 malachite in the stockpile) are derived from `settled` (no extra run); `carveMine` in `tools/make_fixtures.js` returns the coordinates.

### Seams for S7 (clay and copper) and S8 (thornback)
- **Where the items come from:** flint, clay and malachite items drop from digging `M.FLINT` / `M.CLAY` / `M.MALACHITE` (`DIG` in `sim/dig.js`); they are ordinary items in `ITEMS`. Use `sim.apply({type:"mine", op:"seek", item})` or the Dig tool to get them. `loadFixture("ores")` has them in the stockpile.
- **Ridge for draft:** `sim.world.geo.ridge[0]` = `{x,y,z}` (stand cell; the primary ridge cell), `[1]` is the alternate; the surface cell below each has `F.RIDGE`. Test with `world.flags(x,y,z-1) & F.RIDGE`.
- **A station on the ground:** `sim.ents.add("furniture", {f:"kiln", x,y,z, owner:0, rv:0, rt:0})` (reservation fields `rv`/`rt` as for racks); add a drawing branch in `render/renderer.js furniture()` and a line in `ui/text.js describe()`; a job that uses it reserves it with `reserve(sim, v, e)` and a verb in `tasks.js` that checks `E(sim, s)`. Use `gain(sim, v, "craft", value, novel, 0.7)` for kiln XP.
- **S8:** `world.geo.caves[0].floor` (stand-cell indices of the hall) and `.cells` (all carved air, including the ramp) are the thornback lair and its approach; `mouth` is the surface entrance. "Restricted cells" can be the seen frontier: `isSeen`, `isFrontier` in `sim/seen.js`.

## S2 sim API (for the UI)
- Commands via `sim.apply`: `{type:"order", ids:[villagerId], verb:"forage|chop|gather|haul|move", tid? | x,y,z?}`, `{type:"keep", item, n}`, `{type:"unkeep", item}`, `{type:"cancel", ids?}`. Items: `berry dried grass stick log`.
- Villager entity (`sim.ents.ofKind("villager")`): traits `t`, needs `hunger thirst energy warm mood hp`, `carry`, `job {k,c,...}`, `why {job, cls, score, parts{base,urg,fit,prox,path,pref,tmp,sw}, rej:[{job,cls,score,blocker}] (top 3), lf (last failure), t}`, `order`, skills `sk`, `rest/slp`. Helpers in `sim/villager.js` (`lifeStage`, `ageDays`, ...) and `sim/skills.js` (`level`).
- Stockpile: `sim.stock` zone + `survey(sim)` from `sim/items.js` -> `{stock, loose, carried, rations}` (per item counts); `sim.board` has `runway`, `fc`/`sc` critical flags, `dem` demand vector and `keeps`. Chronicle: `sim.chron.entries` / `sim.chron.recs`.

## Layout
`src/sim/` is pure (no DOM, no real time, no Math.random); the UI and renderer change the sim only through `sim.apply(cmd)`. The bundler supports `export const a = ...` one name per line, `export function/class`, and `import { a, b } from "./x.js"` only.

## UI structure (S3) and seams for S4
- `src/main.js` builds one `game` object `{sim, cam, renderer, state, ui, modes, ...}`; panels and input read `game.sim` and change it only with `game.sim.apply(cmd)`. `game.setSim(sim)` swaps the world (load / new game). `ui` is player-view state (selection, hover, overlays, targeting) and is never saved.
- `src/ui/text.js` is pure (no DOM): blocker / job / class text, `explain(sim, v)` (the Why record in sentences), `alerts`, `warnings`. `tests/ui_text.js` covers it.
- Villager names are generated in the sim (`makeName` at founding / birth, stored on the entity, so they are in saves); lineage and parents come from `v.lin`, `v.par`, `chron.recs`.
- **Modes:** `ui.mode` is `"select"` or `"build"`; switch with `game.setMode(name)` (calls `modes[name].enter/leave`). `game.modes.build` (`ui/build.js`) = `{enter, leave, down, move, up, draw}`: `up` returning true consumes the click; `down` must return false to keep `ui.press` alive (the mode keeps its own drag in `ui.build`). Esc cancels a drag/stamp first, then returns to select. `render/overlays.js` calls `modes[ui.mode].draw`; planned pieces are drawn for every mode by `render/ghosts.js`. S4b (possession) can register `game.modes.possess` the same way and must yield WASD in `ui/input.js`.
- **Possess hook:** the inspector (`ui/inspector.js`, `villagerHtml`) has a disabled `Possess` button with `data-act`; WASD pan keys live in `ui/input.js` (`PAN_KEYS`, `panKeys`) and would need to yield to possession input.

## Discovery & fire (S7a)
GDD §15, §11.8, §11.9, §10 (familiarity). Fire is **found, not researched**: nobody knows it at the start; lightning strikes a tree in the first autumn, whoever sees it burn may understand fire, and from then on fire rings can be built and tended. S7b adds the clay -> charcoal -> copper chain on top of the same engine.

- **Heat function** (`sim/heat.js`, K8/§11.9, pure): `P = H x E x D`, `H` fuel (brush 1.0, wood 1.6, charcoal 2.4, coal 3.0), `E = 1 + 0.1 x sum(ins)` over the five faces (four sides + above; open 0, wood 0.4, soil/stone 0.6, daub 0.8, lined 1.0, lid-with-vent 0.5), `D` = product of bellows 1.3, vent 1.25, windy ridge 1.3, smoke-bound 0.7, sealed 0.5, capped at 1.5. Levels at P >= 0.4 / 1.2 / 2.1 / 4.0 / 5.5 (Warm, Hearth, Kiln, Furnace, Forge); wood faces cap at 2. `tests/heat.js` lists every layout the GDD names (open fire 2, lined pit 3, four layouts reach 4, the sealed closet drops to 2, wood tops out at 3.48). `stationHeat(sim, e, fuel)` reads the same inputs from the cells around a station entity.
- **Data** (`sim/phenomena.js`): `PHENOMENA` rows (needs: input tags, heat source `src`, `heat {min,max}`, `enclosed`, `duration`, `verb`; salience, `serves` pressure tags, what it `opens`, `produces`, `nearMiss` hint text that never names the product, `curio` text, `first()` chronicle line, `stub`), `CRAFTS` (name, era, what it opens, the **evidence** that makes a lost craft cheap to find again), `ERAS` (Hearth -> Hide -> Stone -> Clay -> Timber -> Copper -> Iron), `TAGS`/`LOOKS`/`TAG_PRESSURE` for materials. `matches(row, ctx)` is the one matcher (`{ok, near, miss}`). FIRE_TENDING is live; BAKE_CLAY, HOLDS_LIQUID, SMOULDER_WOOD, KILN_FIRING and SMELT_COPPER are complete stubs the engine ignores until `stub` is removed.
- **Knowledge** (`sim/knowledge.js`): `v.fam = {CRAFT: 0..1}`; **a craft is known while a living villager has f > 0** (`can(sim, craft)`, checked when a job is chosen; skills never gate it). Discoverer 0.5, witness 0.2 (D8). Watching: a learner within 4 cells of a practitioner at work gains `0.025 x aptitude x (1 - f)` per 100 ticks (x2 child beside a parent); Teach (S5's command) gives x3 for every craft the teacher holds at >= 0.3; using a craft nudges f up; f < 0.3 wastes 20% of fuel. The last holder dying logs a LOSS ("only X knows" at one holder) and the era label can regress. `sim.disc` (saved + hashed) holds the village-level state (seen, dead ends, near-miss counts, Curiosities, lost crafts, pressure tags, event cooldowns, witness dedup, station cooldowns, the Investigate request, the trace).
- **Pressure** (`warmRaw`, `updatePressure`): tags `warm`, `keep_food`, `carry_water`, `tool_edge`, smoothed daily. Warm pressure is 0 before day 50 of the first year (grace), then `max(coldAhead, 0.5 x winterAhead, share of cold villagers)`.
- **Systemic events** (`sim/events.js`, §11.8): rows with a pressure tag, threshold, days, daily chance, cooldown and an `absent(sim)` precondition. `eventDue(row, st, input)` is the pure decision (`tests/events.js` runs it over a year of pressure on 40 seeds, no sim). **Lightning**: pressure(warm) > 0.3 for 2 days with fire unknown and nothing burning, then 40%/day (stream `event.lightning`); it picks a tree 8-14 cells from camp (stream `event.pick`), which burns 2 days as a `flame` entity, falls in as a charred **stump** and smoulders 2 more. First strike in 40 seeds: days 58-67 (winter is day 75). **Stump**: tool_edge > 0.3 for two seasons and no charcoal; it sleeps until S7b feeds that pressure. Game menu > Force lightning / Force smouldering stump sends `{type:"force", ev}`.
- **Witness** (`sim/discovery.js siteTick/observe`): every 50 ticks each running fire is a phenomenon; every awake villager within 12 cells rolls once per (phenomenon, site, villager) per 450 ticks: `salience x attention (.3 passive, .6 working, 1 performing) x (0.6 + 0.4 x intelligence) x (1 + pressure) x jitter(0.8-1.2, stream "witness")`, sticks at >= 0.2. Nobody learning it leaves a Curiosity. The roll and its factors are in the trace (`sim.disc.trace`, last 40; Almanac toggle). Seeds 1-6 forced at day 15: someone understood within 125 ticks every time.
- **Tinker** (`jobs.js genTinker`, `discovery.js`): a normal board job, **leisure** class when unasked (and only while there is an itch: pressure > 0.3, a Curiosity or a stranger material in stock), **standing** when asked (`{type:"investigate", ids, item?}` / Almanac button: 1-3 villagers for 3 days). The village keeps at most **8 candidate experiments** (material in reach x station context x verb `heat hit soak bury mix press bind leave` (fuel is one family per station: put it on the fire), weight `1 + 2 x relevance + novelty`, relevance 1 for a Curiosity or lost-craft evidence at that station; cached until stock, stations, dead ends or the day change); one experiment per station per day, at most 3 tinkerers; an attempt is 300 ticks, consumes the material, and either fires a phenomenon (performer attention 1.0, +0.3 craft XP) or records a **dead end** keyed to the heat level tried (invalidated when the station gets hotter), or a near-miss counted toward the Almanac hint (after 3). A craft nobody knows with **evidence** standing weights the matching experiment x8 (and counts as an itch of its own): measured 0.25x the expected tries of finding it from scratch, 7.5 -> 1.9 (gate 0.4x).
- **Fire** (`sim/fire.js`): `hearth` furniture `{fuel, lit, fi, hl}` (4 sticks, built like a bed as craft_site; needs a living knower of FIRE_TENDING to be placed); villagers build one themselves when they know fire and the cold is coming (`sim/hearth.js autoHearth`); **tend_fire** is a standing-class chore (2 workers; takes logs/sticks from the stockpile, derived haul/chop when short; `UNKNOWN_CRAFT` blocker if the holders are all dead) that keeps it above 1200 ticks of fuel while a fire is wanted. A stick burns 900 ticks, a log 2400, a hearth holds 3600. A lit hearth adds up to +0.30 (by heat level) to anyone within 5 cells, fading with distance, and +0.20-0.30 to everyone in the same enclosed room (a hearth deep in a closed room with no door within 3 cells is smoke-bound, level 1); villagers who are cold huddle at the fireside. Raw berries and tubers eaten within 8 cells of a lit hearth give x1.3 (cooking). 30 days from day 71 on 4 seeds: 3 cold deaths with fire vs 11 without. Hearth age appears in the chronicle on discovery.
- **UI**: Almanac button + panel (era, crafts known with holders and strength words, lost crafts, Curiosities with a near-miss hint, "tried, nothing came of it", Investigate, debug trace toggle); inspector shows each villager's crafts and familiarity; flames, smoke and a glowing fire ring on the map (animated, firelight at night); the Warmth overlay shows fire warmth (orange glow with `+0.30`) and room warmth including the fire; Fire ring tool in build mode (greyed out until someone knows fire); Why text for `tend_fire`, `tinker` and the new blockers `UNKNOWN_CRAFT`, `NO_CANDIDATE`, `COOLDOWN`.
- **Saves**: version 6 (`fam`, `disc`, `flame` entities, `stump` species). v2-v5 migrate with nobody knowing anything.
- **Tests** (quick set): `heat`, `events`, `discovery` (forced lightning -> witness -> known, tinker route, tinker job consumes material + dead end + cooldown, unasked = leisure and itch, loss -> blocked tend_fire -> evidence discount, hearth chore + warmth + closed room, autonomy builds a hearth, placement needs a knower, watching/kin/Teach, cooking, S7b seam, save round-trip and determinism). Fixture `fire` (two holders, a lit hearth with fuel, logs and sticks, day 74).

## Clay, charcoal & copper (S7b)
GDD §15.5, §15.7 rows 5-7 and 9, §11.9, §14.3-14.4, §12.4-12.5, Appendix B. The chain fire -> pottery -> charcoal -> copper runs on S7a's engine: every link is a phenomenon row, found by witness or by tinker, never by a flag.

- **Phenomena** (`sim/phenomena.js`, all five live): BAKE_CLAY (clay, heat 2+ at a hearth/pit/flame, 600 ticks), HOLDS_LIQUID (craft **VESSELS**, a new craft: baked clay soaked), SMOULDER_WOOD (wood, sealed or buried, heat exactly 1, 1200), KILN_FIRING (clay, pit, heat 3+, 900), SMELT_COPPER (malachite + carbon, pit, heat 4+, 1200). Crafts POTTERY (Clay age), VESSELS, CHARCOAL, KILN, SMELTING (Copper age). Hints and Curiosity lines never name a product (`tests/chain.js` greps them).
- **Routes.** Pottery: witness (wet clay within 4 cells of a lit fire of heat 2+ for 600 ticks bakes one lump: `clayByFire` in `discovery.js`), tinker (clay left at a hearth/kiln), or the first kiln batch. Charcoal: witness a smouldering stump (the systemic event, or the fallen burning tree), tinker (wood **buried** under a fire = a smothered hearth, or left in a mound). Vessels: someone drinks beside baked clay (`waterTouch`, called by the drink verb), or tinker (baked clay soaked). Copper: tinker only (malachite + charcoal at a kiln that reaches 4), then everyone watching rolls to understand it. **Two routes to heat 4**: a kiln with a **bellows** beside it, or a kiln on the **windy ridge** (`geo.ridge`); the lidded-vent route is in the heat table but there is no lid piece yet.
- **Tinker changes.** An experiment may carry a companion fuel (`item + with`, e.g. malachite + charcoal): the heat comes from the fuel (`candHeat`, station `hf` = level by fuel). `leave` and `bury` last 1200 ticks of game time but cost the tinkerer only `TINKER_WORK` = 300 ticks of labour (a long burn is set up and left, so a sleepy village still finishes it). The pool of 8 is a weighted draw from everything possible, redrawn each day by a hash of (candidate, day) (no RNG use, so previews never move the stream); weights: Curiosity at the spot x4 (only for the stuff it is about), a stranger material (clay, malachite, baked clay, until its craft is known) x2, camp experiments x0.5, evidence x8. **Investigate** takes `at` (a hearth/kiln/mound id): "try things HERE" (inspector button "Investigate here"). While an investigation is asked a fire is wanted (so a hearth is lit in summer too).
- **Stations** (furniture through the csite pipeline, craft work): `kiln` (clay-lined pit, 4 clay, its four sides count as lined), `mound` (earth-covered pit, 4 dirt: sealed, 1.6 x 1.3 x 0.5 = 1.04, a smoulder), `workbench` (2 logs, always available), `bellows` (2 fired clay), `waterjar` (1 jar). `PLACE_NEEDS` in `sim/build.js` hides kiln/bellows until POTTERY or KILN is known, the mound until CHARCOAL, the jar until VESSELS (the palette never spoils what is still to find; the sim also refuses with `unknown`). `sim/craft.js` holds the data (`RECIPES`) and the batch logic; `heat.js stationFaces` knows kiln and mound; `heatReport` says why a fire is not hotter.
- **Recipes** (`RECIPES`): fired clay (4 clay + 4 sticks -> 4, kiln, heat 3), jars (same, 2 jars, needs VESSELS), charcoal (4 logs -> 3, mound), copper (4 malachite + 2 charcoal -> 2 ingots, kiln at heat 4, 1200 ticks, difficulty 0.9), copper pick (2 ingots + 1 log, workbench, 600 ticks). Fired clay and jars burn at difficulty 0.7. A kiln or mound batch is loaded by hand trip by trip (`load`), lit (`light`) and then burns by itself; when it ends the products drop loose at the station (hauled by the baseline), the lighter earns craft XP for what came out, and the matching phenomena fire with the lighter performing (a kiln run teaches KILN_FIRING, a smelt teaches SMELTING).
- **Jobs** (standing class, derived): `fire_kiln`, `burn_charcoal`, `smelt`, `make_tool` exist only while a keep-N on the product is refilling (the player's request), or a keep-N on something that needs it is short (copper needs charcoal; a copper pick needs copper: depth-1). One worker per recipe. Blockers: `UNKNOWN_CRAFT`, `NO_STATION`, `STATION_BUSY`, `TOO_COLD` (with the reason in the Why panel and the station inspector), `NO_MATERIAL` (names the item). Digging, clay and ore stay the player's orders (R8). `fetch_water` (demand class) fills a water jar below half; a thirsty villager drinks at a jar with water when it is nearer than the stream by 6 steps (`drink:jar`).
- **Quality** (§14.4): `0.5 + 0.5 x craft level + room offset` (bench inside a Workshop only; offset = (room quality - 0.5) x 0.3, capped at +-0.15). Stored on the product stack (`q`, averaged when stacks merge, kept in hands). It sets a pick's durability (40-120 digs).
- **The copper pick** is a capability, not a bonus: made by a new job at a bench, taken from the stockpile by a digger (`equip` step before `dig`; held in `v.eq = {item, u (uses left), q}`), dig rate x1.6 while held (`craft.toolRate`, the only effect), wears out per cell dug. S8 arming seam: `v.eq` is the equipment slot.
- **Workshop** purpose now needs a workbench (a checklist line `bench`, with a hint to build one).
- **Pressure** (`knowledge.js`): `tool_edge` = 0.4 x min(1, digs today / 10) + 0.35 if malachite is in the stockpile + 0.2 once hard rock was dug; `carry_water` = twice the thirsty share. The stump event reads tool_edge (it fires on days 70-100 for a village that digs and holds ore, never before two seasons; `tests/events.js`).
- **Validator** (`sim/geology.js`): also `kilnsite` (>= 2 flat 4x4 blocks within 14 cells of the camp with room for a bellows on every side) and `stumptree` (trees for the stump event within 40 cells), on top of clay, flint, malachite, wood, ridge and the cave. The seeds that re-roll are unchanged (13, 14, 16, 18).
- **UI**: build palette (stations appear when known), stock bars (products appear once someone knows how to make them, or one exists), station inspector (state, heat by fuel with P, layout, "To get hotter: ..." advice, what it can make and why not, Investigate here), water jar line, Almanac era flow "Hearth -> Clay -> Copper" plus Vessels/Charcoal/Kiln firing crafts and "tried X with Y" dead ends, new Why text for the five job kinds, map drawings for every station (fire in a lit kiln, smoke over a mound, a jar with its water line).
- **Saves**: still version 6 (station and `v.eq` fields are additive; `disc` gains `ex dg hd worn`, read lazily).
- **Fixtures**: `hearth` (fire known by two, a lit hearth, `settled` + prepFire), `clay` (pottery known by adults 0 and 2, clay 12, dirt 8), `copperready` (charcoal 10, malachite 12, fired clay 4, a kiln on flat ground with no draft, nobody knows smelting). Derived fixtures are now written alone when only they are missing (`loadFixture`).
- **Tests** (quick set): `tests/chain.js` (the table, stations, witness x3, tinker x7, informed investigate, stations build and produce, both heat-4 routes through the board (and the blocked no-draft case), loss and rediscovery near a kiln, ingot -> `make_tool`, pick effect and wear, workshop, jars, stump pressure, save round-trip mid-burn, text), `tests/property6.js` (the competent bot in segments of <= 10 days: fire 0.0, pottery 2.4, charcoal 0.5, smelting 4.2, pick 6.4 game days), `tests/events.js` (stump), `tests/geology.js` (new counts). `ui_smoke` places a kiln, builds it and opens its inspector. **--full only: `tests/bots/chainrun.js`** (start -> copper pick with the competent bot, one seed, <= 1 game year): not run by the author; it is for the balance pass.

## Creatures, expeditions & combat (S8)
The last stage of C3. Fauna, fear, the melee verb, the thornback's lair, one expedition type with recall, and possession combat. Everything goes through the existing pipelines: one Action runner, blocker enums for every refusal, `sim.apply` for every player input, no new randomness outside named streams (`fauna`, `melee`).

- **Fauna (`sim/fauna.js`)**: rows in `FAUNA` (hp, speed, aggression, yields) + a tiny behaviour per row. A creature is a `fauna` entity (`sp, hp, mhp, cd, st, home, ak, ht/hd` = last hit for the flash); `sim.wild = {init, tbd}`. They are placed the first time the sim steps (10 hoppers on the meadow, the thornback in `world.geo.caves[0]`), so old saves and fixtures get them with no version bump. **Hopper**: wanders, bolts in short bursts from villagers within 4 cells, breeds slowly (a daily chance when two are near, up to 14), huntable. A kill drops 2 `hopper_meat` (2 rations each, spoils 12%/day, cooked x1.5 near a lit fire). **Thornback**: 150 HP, patrols within 6 of its home, hunts anyone inside its territory (and within 5 cells outside it, leashed at 17), bites 40 (60%, every 45 ticks), retreats below 30% HP, regenerates 2 HP/100 ticks once left alone, leaves carrion (6 meat); after 25-30 days a new one moves in (chronicled).
- **Health and wounds**: hits reduce `hp`. A wounded villager (< 85%) heals +6/day, +12 more at rest; below half health `mend` (a personal need, Why text "wounded") sends them to rest. Death by thornback is ordinary permadeath (`kill(..., "thornback")`) with a chronicle context ("mauled by the thornback in its cave, while guided").
- **Fear (`fauna.fearStep`, every 10 ticks)**: rises with how close a scary creature is (within 14 cells, +-3 levels), falls over time (faster in company), a bite sets it to 0.8. Above `FEAR_AT` 0.6 an autonomous villager gets the **`flee` job** at personal-critical class (it outranks every other need) to camp or away from the beast, and keeps at it until fear < 0.3. With the beast at their elbow and nowhere to run they `defend` (strike back). A guided villager is **Steadfast** (`possess.steadfast(v)`): no flee candidate, and no restricted-cell check.
- **Restricted cells (`sim/lair.js`)**: the lair territory (the chamber floor widened by 4 cells, 2 levels below to 4 above the floor) and cave cells nobody has seen. `plan()` checks the Task a candidate would build (`unsafeTask`): any stand or dig cell inside is refused with the blocker **`UNSAFE`** (Why panel: "too dangerous to do on their own...") and the target goes on the villager's bad list. Idle wandering avoids them; delegated crews' searches skip ore inside the territory.
- **The melee verb**: one `strike` verb in `tasks.js` + `fauna.swing(att, tgt)` (hit chance, damage scaled by strength, hardiness and fitness, +spear). The AI uses it for `hunt` (an order on a hopper via right-click, or a demand-class job while food is short) and `defend`; the player uses the same Task (`possess.interact` on an adjacent creature). A **copper spear** (bench recipe: 1 copper + 1 stick, `RECIPES.copper_spear`, a `keep` makes it) is taken into `v.eq` by the `equip` step (AI hunters grab one from the stockpile; a guided villager presses T in the stockpile); thrust 50 (three kill the thornback), bare hands 12, 30 uses.
- **Possession combat (`ui/possess.js`, `render/`)**: E or left-click on an adjacent creature strikes; the banner shows health (guidance ends below 10%), fear (with the 60% line, "steadfast") and what is in hand; hits flash a red ring and a floating number ("miss" for a whiff); underground (rock overhead) everything beyond ~6 cells of the guided villager is dark (`overlays.drawLight`, render-side only). Digging is the existing `dig` verb.
- **Expeditions (`sim/expedition.js`, `ui/expedition.js`)**: `{type:"expedition", op:"start", ids, item: flint|clay|malachite|any}` / `op:"recall"`. Members get `v.xp`; each runs an **order-class** `mine` job on its own S6 *seek* designation (up to three, from three anchors round camp; `exp` and `deep` fields on the designation: ore >= 3 levels down, not in the territory). They are ordinary villagers: they eat, sleep, flee and refuse UNSAFE cells, then carry on. The recall rule (checked every 20 ticks) is: **found** (any search got its sample), **a member below half health**, **a member hungrier than 30%**, **2.5 days**, or the Recall button; then they carry the loose sample to the stockpile and walk to camp, and a report goes to the chronicle (what was brought, how deep, who was lost) with a "Send again" button. The card shows members, target, status, found/depth, time left and the stall reason (`UNSAFE` while someone is afraid, `NO_PATH`, `NO_TARGET`). New ore seen by the crew and the first sight of each creature enter `sim.disc.curio` with `wild: 1` (shown in the Almanac; never an itch to tinker).
- **UI**: creatures are drawn (a tan hopper with ears, a dark spiked thornback with a health bar once hurt), picked by proximity, with an inspector card; right-click a hopper = Hunt; right-click the thornback tells you villagers will not fight it; the Expeditions panel appears when villagers are selected or a crew is out; the Why panel explains flee / defend / mend / UNSAFE / the crew's order.
- **Fixtures**: `thornback` (from `settled`: the thornback on the meadow 18 cells east of camp with its own home, a copper spear in stock) and `expedition` (from `settled`: creatures placed, berries in stock, crew = the first three adults; `prepExpedition` also prepares a fresh sim of any other seed).

## Living world: farming, husbandry, preservation, lifecycles (S10)

## Underground and world: cave-in, bad air, light, biomes, settings (S12)
GDD §13.6 (R20 hazards), §13 light, §11.4 biomes, §22 accessibility, §20 ironman (OD-12). Phase 3, features only; every number is a best guess (D29).

- **Cave-in** (`sim/underground.js` answers, `sim/cavein.js` acts). A stand cell under *natural* rock (dirt, soft or hard rock, ore, rubble) is **unstable** when no solid cell stands within `SPAN_R = 2` cells of it on its level: rock wall, a pillar, a timber support, rubble. A one-wide tunnel and a hall up to 4 wide hold; 5x5 cracks at its middle, 7x7 in the middle 3x3. Natural caves (`world.geo.caves`), open pits (no roof within two cells), built roofs and tall halls are exempt. `world.ugd` logs changed cells; `ugTick` (every 20 ticks) re-checks only the cells around them, keeps `sim.ug.cr = [[cell, firstTick]]` (saved + hashed) and, `CRACK_TICKS = 1200` after the first crack, `collapse()` fills the cracked cell and the one above with `M.RUBBLE` (dig row: stone), buries loose items, hurts everyone under it (`COLLAPSE_DMG` 40, death cause "a cave-in") and sets them down on the nearest open cell. **Supports** are `M.SUPPORT` cells, built as the wall material `support` (`BMAT.support`: 2 timber, needs FRAMING): the palette lists "Timber support" under Wall once someone knows framing. The **mine planner** (`mine.queueFrom`) leaves a pillar (`d.pil`) wherever digging a cell would exceed the span, so a region or a followed vein never makes a hall that would fall. `ugAuto` (every 100 ticks) plans one support at the middle of the cracked cells when the village has timber and framing; building it is the one job villagers do under a cracking roof (jobs.js exception).
- **Bad air.** `airMap(sim)` (cached on `world.version` and the vents): the network of open stand cells under rock (cells ever changed, plus the cave), steps from fresh air (a cell touching the open, a `vent` or a `bellows` furniture). A *connected* dead end more than `VENT_N = 12` steps in goes stale (natural caves are exempt; a sealed hall is left alone). Worldgen puts six gas pockets (3x3 of rock 7-9 below the surface, `world.geo.gas`) and one group of cells at the far end of the cave: once open, everything within 2 cells that is more than `POCKET_N = 4` steps from fresh air is foul. In foul air a villager loses `AIR_DMG` 90 hp and 1.5 energy a day (`villager.js updateNeeds`, death cause "bad air"). A **Vent** piece (4 sticks, no craft needed), a bellows nearby, or a second opening to the surface clears it. `ugAuto` plans a vent beside foul air somebody has seen (the one job allowed in foul air).
- **Autonomy refuses what it knows** (`lair.restricted` now also answers `"unstable"` and `"badair"`; blocker `UNSAFE`, text updated): cells within one of a cracking cell, and foul cells that have been SEEN (fog). ## People depth: rust, hobbies, genotype, relationships, grief, graves (S13)
- **Skill rust** (`skills.js`): `sk[s].lu` (last worked) and `sk[s].rs` (share of the level lost, 0..0.3). After `RUST_GRACE` 15 idle days `rustDay` (daily) moves `rs` toward 30% with an e-folding of 40 days; a skill used in the last day shakes 40% of it off per day (back in about five days). `level()` = `peakLevel()` x (1 - rs); XP is never touched and **familiarity (`fam`) never rusts**. At level 0.5 `titleCheck` writes a chronicle note and awards "renowned <doer>".
- **Hidden genotype and inbreeding (R16)**: `v.g = {trait: [allele, allele]}`; a child gets one allele from each parent (+-5% noise, a 4% sport on the pair) and the visible trait is their settled blend (`inheritGeno`; `inheritTraits` is the same function). Founders, clones and migrated saves get alleles from `genoFrom` (a stable hash split around the trait). `v.gp` remembers the grandparents, so `people.js kinship()` reaches first cousins from `v.par`/`v.gp` alone (0.5 parent/child/full sibling, 0.25 half-sibling/uncle/grandparent, 0.125 cousin). A child of kin is **weak** with chance `1.2 x kinship` (`v.wk`, -12% hardiness, -15% fertility, -7% longevity, one more trait -10%, named in the birth line). Partner choice (`pickPartner`, used by `breedDaily` and the breed bot) weights `1 - 0.8 x kinship`, x2 for a friend, x4 for a partner; a bonded pair that is near kin is passed over half the time. The inspector's Kin section lists relatives with the word for each.
- **Relationships** (`bonds.js`, low-level so skills/jobs can read it): `v.aff[other] = [work, hard, clash]` points, `v.mn[other]` Teach history, `v.rel` the tiers (max 6, D8): partner (`v.mate`), mentor (8 paying lessons, `noteLesson` from `skills.gain`; the mentor's lessons are 10% stronger), friend (30 points), rival (-30). `socialTick` (every 100 ticks) adds `2 x chemistry` for pairs working within 3 cells (chemistry from drive/leisure/intelligence differences and a per-pair knack: positive builds friendship, negative builds clash) and 0.5 for cold huddlers; a death that is not old age gives +8 hardship points to adults within 10 cells. `peopleDaily` fades points 3% a day and refreshes tiers with hysteresis. **Effects**: `jobs.js scoreCand` has one more additive term `soc` (+5 next to a working friend/partner, -5 next to a rival, inside the within-class clamp; shown as "Company" in Why with a note naming them); a partner without a bed sleeps in the partner's; partners are the first pick to breed.

At death it goes to the partner, then children (eldest first), full siblings, then bonds; if all of them already carry one, it is buried in the grave. ## Endless world (S16)

The land is unbounded in x and y (signed integer columns); z stays 0..23. Nothing is generated up front: terrain is a **pure function of (seed, coordinates)**, the sim holds only the chunks people are near or have changed, and everything that used to assume a 128 x 128 grid now works on coordinates.

**Modules.**
- `sim/cellkey.js`: a cell index is one exact double, `((z * 2^20) + (y + 2^19)) * 2^20 + (x + 2^19)`; chunk keys are `(cx + 32768) * 65536 + (cy + 32768)`. `World.idx` / `World.xyz` keep their names, so every `Set` of cells in the sim still works; what could not survive is an *array indexed by a cell* (path scratch, `seen`, mine scratch, the air network): those are Maps or per-search node numbers now.
- `sim/noise.js`: integer value noise over hashed lattice points (`vnoise`, `hash2`, `hash3`); no sin/exp/random.
- `sim/terrain.js`: `genChunk(def, cx, cy)` -> `{mats, flgs, hs, bm}` (16 x 16 x 24), **order independent**: no whole-map pass. Height and biome are noise; **rivers** are the 128-contour of a noise field (distance from the field's gradient, tapering out under a slow mask); **lakes** are basins where a slow noise is high; **clay** banks sit on the shore; **strata** and **flint** (2.4% of the soft rock), **iron / coal** (flat veins, shallow band and deep band), **malachite** blobs (their cores run a seam up to a green tell) are per-cell hashes and noise thresholds per depth band; **ridges** are strict local height maxima over 5 x 5 (the 2-cell halo is computed too); **natural caves** are worms seeded per 64 x 64 region (a ramp from a hillside, then a roaming tunnel with chambers), each chunk checks the worms of the regions round it; **gas pockets** (S12) and the S14 **salt / peat / herb** deposits are per-region seeds checked from the neighbours. New flag bits: `NATURAL` (cave air: its vault holds), `GAS` (rock that releases bad air), `NOGROW` (no flora).
- `sim/spawn.js`: `defOf(seed, opts)` = the world's definition (cached). It searches outward for the camp: the origin, then rings of eight points every 28 cells, the first that passes `validateSpawn` (clay, flint, malachite, iron, coal, wood, a ridge, kiln sites, stump trees: two separate sources each within 64 cells, a reachable cave, and the S14 checks). **Moving the spawn replaces re-rolling the seed**; 20 of 20 seeds find a fair start, 12 at the origin and the rest within 3 rings. The slice-critical content is **placed**, stamped by a pure rule keyed to the spawn: a lake with a stream (clay banks on it), two malachite stains with a seam to the surface, and the cave with the thornback's lair, a malachite face and foul air at its far end (`def.stamps`, applied last by `genChunk`).
- `sim/flora.js`: `floraOf(def, chunk)` rolls trees, berries, tufts and tubers from a chunk-keyed RNG stream (`Rng(seed ^ ..., chunkKey)`), so a chunk regrows exactly what it grew.
- `sim/chunks.js`: activation and unloading (below). `sim/world.js`: the chunk store.

**World store.** `World.chunks` holds the *resident* chunks (activated, or changed): authoritative, in a Map. `World.gc` is a bounded LRU (256) of regrowable chunks, so a read of far, untouched land generates it without making it part of the sim. Only `set` / `setFlags` make a chunk resident. `genCount` counts generations. The renderer reads through its **own** view and cache (`render/renderer.js`, 2400 chunks, least recently used first, ~31 MB): it shows the resident chunks as they are and everything else from `genChunk`, and never makes anything resident.

**Activation** (`sim.step`, every 20 ticks, ascending chunk key): every chunk within `ACT_CH` (3) chunks of an *anchor's* chunk is made resident and grown. Anchors: villagers (so expedition crews), the camp, the stockpile, mine designations. **The camera is not an anchor; the sim never depends on it.** Far from the camp (>= 56 cells) a freshly activated chunk may hold wild creatures (hoppers; a wolf pack in the highland, grazers in the marsh with the S14 content), drawn from the chunk's own stream and marked `wi`. **Unloading** (every 200 ticks): an active chunk with nothing changed (no cell written; no entity added, removed or moved in other than wildlife) more than `DEACT_CH` (4) chunks from every anchor loses its plants and wild creatures and is dropped; it regrows identically if anyone comes back. A *changed* chunk is never dropped. Plants that only grew or were picked from are not "changes" and start over. `chunk.last` is the tick the chunk was last within reach of an anchor (the **S17 seam** for dormancy); `chunkMeta` (key, active, changed, last) is saved and hashed.

**Fog and the unexplored.** The surface is "seen" within `SPAWN_SIGHT` (40) of the camp from the start and within `SURFACE_SIGHT` (14) of where a villager has been (re-done when they cross into a new 4 x 4 block). Surface columns revealed are a list (`seen.cols`, saved as run-length pairs, replayed from the terrain *as generated*); `seen.b` is a Map of per-chunk byte arrays; `seen.bbox` is the explored box. Land nobody has been near is **drawn dim** (a bluish, darker rendering of the real terrain with its biome tint: the world's shape is visible, no creatures, no marks); underground stays fully fogged (black). Key `0` frames the explored box.

**Every fixed-size assumption** was replaced: `W` / `H` are gone (a grep for `\bW\b` and `\bH\b` in `src/` finds only key-cap letters and local height helpers named for the halo / spawn), `geo.hs` is `world.hsAt(x, y)`, `geo.bm` is `world.biomeAt(x, y)`, `geo.caves` is the placed cave only (`geo.ridge` the two nearest ridge cells), natural caves and gas are flags, S14 deposits are `deposits(sim, kind, x, y, r)` / `depositsIn(def, ...)`. Pathing numbers nodes per search (a Map from cell to node, arrays by node; same 4000-node budget; an unreachable goal now reads `BUDGET`, not `NO_PATH`, because the land is not finite), the mine tunnel planner, reach map, air network and rooms flood fill use Maps and signed coordinates, the camera has no clamp, the renderer's `tops` is sized to the view, plant caps and seed rain (`REGION_R` 62), hopper caps, drink spots and the "this world" facts are regional (round the camp), and plants age only where people are about (inside the region and a live chunk). A wander target of "none" is `NO_T` (not -1: the land has negative coordinates), a task step with "no place" has `z = -1` (not `x = -1`).

**Saves.** `SAVE_VERSION` 9: seed, camp, `chunks` (the resident ones), deltas (cells that differ from `genChunk`), all entities, `seen = {c, l}`. Unmodified land is not saved. **A save of the old fixed map (v < 9) is refused with a plain message** ("This save was made in the old fixed-size world ... Start a new game."): its edits would land on the wrong land (decision D33). `restore` re-grows each saved active chunk's trees' obstacle bits from the seed, then lays the deltas over it.

**Test hooks.** `?spawn=64,64` in the page URL, `opts.spawn` in `Sim.create` / `generateWorld`, pin the camp (a save records it); `WORLD.prefer` (set by `tests/legacy.js`) tries a position first and keeps it only if it passes the validator.

**Numbers** (this machine): `genChunk` 0.3 ms; a resident chunk is 12.8 KB of typed arrays (+ its plants, ~50 entities); one game day at the camp with 7 villagers 0.06 ms/tick; the path microbench (`tests/path_bench.js`) is unchanged in kind (100 walkers: search p95 0.2 ms, 0.23 ms/tick). Walking 2000 cells east (`tests/endless.js`): peak 241 resident chunks with 4 anchors (each holds at most a 9 x 9 block), the cache 191, heap 23 MB, typed arrays +7 MB, 69k surface columns explored, a 443 KB save.

**Seams for S17.**
- *Dormancy LOD*: `chunk.last` and `chunkTick`; `activeAt(x, y)` is the one question the fauna tick asks (a creature outside active land does nothing today, S17 can simulate it coarsely); `floraDaily` already gates on "live chunk and inside the region".
- *Save compaction*: the entity list is the bulk of a save (plants of active chunks are saved whole). Plants in a chunk nobody changed could be dropped from a save and regrown from `floraOf` like the cells; `seen.cols` is already run-length.
- *Minimap*: `seen.bbox`, `seen.cs` (per-chunk revealed-column bits) and `renderer.view` (`RenderView`, a cache of `genChunk` output with `hs` / `bm` per column) are everything it needs; a 1-pixel-per-column surface layer can come from `hs` and `bm` without cells.
- *Exploration UX*: the dim rendering is `renderer.draw`'s `dimFill` branch; "unexplored" is `!exploredAt(seen, x, y)`; the frontier is `seen.bbox`.
- *Pathing*: `Pathfinder.find` is still the one entry point; a chunk-level region graph slots in at its top (unbounded land makes it more useful: far goals cost the whole budget today).

**Screenshots** (from `tools/ui_smoke.js`): `tmp/ui-s16-unexplored.png` (300 cells from the camp: dim land with rivers, lakes and the highland tint), `tmp/ui-s16-far-villager.png` (a villager 100+ cells out), `tmp/ui-s16-wide.png` (zoomed out: the explored area bright round the path, the world's shape dim beyond). Known rough edges: a cave mouth cut into the surface can leave one dark cell where the revealed top and the real top differ; the cell card of unexplored land says "Nobody has seen this cell yet" (it is drawn, but not known).

## Endless world: dormancy, saves, minimap, exploration (S17)

**Dormant chunks** (`sim/dormant.js`, driven by `chunks.js`). A changed chunk (it is never dropped) more than `DEACT_CH` chunks from every anchor falls asleep (checked every 100 ticks): `chunk.dz` is the tick, its plants and loose item stacks are packed into `chunk.pk` / `chunk.pi` (arrays, no longer entities: nothing iterates them), its untamed `wi` creatures are removed, and `fireTick`, `spoilDaily` and `faunaTick` skip dormant ground (`World.dormantAt`, `activeAt` is false there). A chunk holding the yard, a field, a pen or the cellar, or a plant / stack that is reserved or has unknown fields, never sleeps. When an anchor comes within `ACT_CH` the chunk wakes and is caught up in closed form: plants are unpacked in order (new ids), growth days added season by season, berries refilled by the berry-season time since the last frost, stumps sprout or rot, saplings grow, the old wither, a burning tree is a stump; flames go out, hearths are out (fuel 0); loose food and hides rot by the elapsed days (winter days at the winter rate); herds are re-rolled from the chunk's own stream salted by the day (`spawnWild`). Not required to equal a never-sleeping chunk; same seed and commands give the same hashes. A sleeping chunk shows no plants or items on the map (they are not entities; a seam: draw `c.pk`).

**Saves** (`SAVE_VERSION` 10; a v9 save loads). Cell deltas per chunk in chunk-local numbers (delta-coded), plants as arrays inside per-chunk groups, the seen set as per-chunk bitsets (`1` = the whole chunk, else the shorter of a run list or 64 hex digits) and per-chunk cell runs, the landmarks seen (`seen.lm`), `chunks` carry `[key, active, changed, last, asleepSince, packedPlants, packedStacks]` for sleepers, `zones`. The hash reads the same canonical forms. Numbers (this machine): seed 7, 7 villagers walking 7 rays of 300-600 cells, 720 chunks resident, 10.5k entities: **1227 KB -> 439 KB** (entities 1122 -> 345 KB, the seen set 87 -> 77 KB); `tests/dormant.js` (7 walkers, 300 cells, 906 chunks resident, 270 changed, 10.5k entities): **481 KB** mid-exploration, about the same at home with 253 chunks asleep; the 2000-cell walk of `tests/endless.js`: 443 -> 156 KB. Three autosave slots of that are 1.4 MB, well inside localStorage (about 5 MB). If a slot cannot be written even after dropping the others (`ui/saves.js autosaveTick`) the player gets a toast and the file downloads (once per three game days; a failed manual save downloads at once; ironman only toasts).

**Cost.** `tests/dormant.js`: ms/tick at a fresh camp with 7 villagers 0.024-0.032, after exploring 906 chunks and coming home (253 asleep, 86 awake, plants and stacks packed) 0.041-0.042; asserted under 1.6x + 0.03 ms and the awake chunk and plant counts bounded. (A tick at the camp is still dominated by the villagers.)

**Minimap** (`ui/minimap.js`, panel `#minimap`, bottom right). 192 px, 1 px = 3 cells by default (wheel: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16). Explored land in colour (top material by height, biome tint, water by depth), everything else dim, from `renderer.view` (its LRU) and the sim's resident chunks as they are, plus `seen.cs`; an explored chunk the render cache no longer holds is regenerated through the render view (at most 48 chunks per redraw, the rest on the next); unexplored land the cache does not hold is dark. It never calls the sim for a chunk (`ui_smoke` asserts the chunk count, `genCount` and resident set do not move while it is clicked, dragged and zoomed). The terrain layer redraws at most every 350 ms (and on a zoom); the overlay (camp diamond, villagers gold / orange on an expedition / cyan scouting with a target cross, landmark icons C S R L, the camera rectangle) every frame. Click or drag = jump the camera; right-click with villagers selected = scout there; the header collapses it; **Home** button and the **Home** key (an `ACTIONS` row, remappable) centre on the camp.

**Scouting and landmarks** (`sim/scout.js`, `sim/landmarks.js`). `{type:"scout", ids, x, y}` is the order verb `scout`: the villagers go in hops of 20 cells to within 3 cells of the target (revealing as they walk, as every villager does), up to 160 cells from camp, and give up after 7 hops that found no path. In the UI: right-click unexplored land with villagers selected (the default order there), the Scout button on the order bar, or right-click the minimap. Landmarks per 64 x 64 region, pure functions of the seed: cave mouth (the cave worms' ramp), salt outcrop (the S14 deposits), ruins (a placeholder: a region hash on dry ground) and a big lake (a probe grid). A villager above ground sees one within `22 + 4 (h - 14)` cells (capped 60) of where they stand, every 100 ticks; it is recorded in `seen.lm`, drawn on the minimap, and 40 or more cells from camp enters the Curiosities (`lm:kind:x,y`, a wild sighting like the animals).

**Spawn water and berries.** The camp gets a small pond (`def.pond`, radius 5, centre 13-14 cells from the camp to the east, south or west by a hash; the lake and its stream stay north where they were, and the terrain code reads it like the lake) so there is drinkable water within about 12 cells (it was 14-22). The spawn search also prefers a camp with water within 14 cells and 3 or more berry bushes within 15 (`validateSpawn` returns `water`, `berries`, `pref`; a preferred spawn `WORLD.prefer` only has to be a fair start): 20 of 20 seeds meet both, 12 at the origin as before, no spawn moved (`tests/spawn17.js`, worldgen only). `WORLD.pond` (default on; `tests/legacy.js` turns it off) and the save's `pond` field say whether a world has the pond, because terrain regrows from the seed: an older save regrows without it. **No fixture was regenerated**: the v9 fixtures load through the v10 loaders (`tests/fixture.js` accepts v9 and up), and `tests/legacy.js` also switches `WORLD.dormant` off so the older, chance-driven tests keep the far land awake as they were tuned on (a dormant chunk changes which wild hoppers exist, and so the fauna stream's draws).

**Stockpile zones** (`sim/yard.js`, the Stockpile build tool). `sim.zones = {n, list:[{id, x0,y0,x1,y1,z, f}]}`; `{type:"zone", op:"add"|"remove"|"filter"}`; at most 8 zones of 12 x 12, every cell free land (walkable, nothing built, no other zone, field, pen or cellar), never over the yard. `f` is `all`, `food` or `mat`. `inStore` counts the zones' cells as stock; `storeCell` prefers a zone whose filter names the item (12 cells of walking), so a yard full of food no longer locks logs and stone out; keep-N caps use `stockCapacity`. Digging and building refuse zone cells like the yard. Every 200 ticks (`yardTick`) a main yard whose free cells have fallen to the food reserve grows by one ring (symmetric, so `CAMP` stays put) if every ring cell is free land, up to 14 a side. Cellars and storage rooms are still `inStore` as before.

**Also fixed.** `Entities` cell keys were `x + 1024 (y + 1024 z)`, which collide for |x| >= 1024: items and plants far out answered for the wrong cell. They are cell indices now.

**Tests.** `tests/dormant.js` (no `legacy.js`: dormancy on; a chunk 150 cells out sleeps and wakes caught up, a save taken asleep wakes identically, two runs agree; ms/tick and save size after exploring), `tests/s17.js` (zones with a filter, the yard ring, the scout order and its determinism, landmarks, lossless save forms), `tests/spawn17.js` (20 seeds), ui_smoke S17 section (minimap visible and bright, click jumps, wheel zooms, drag pans, collapses, nothing in the sim changes; Home key; right-click scout; Stockpile tool by dragging). ## First-person view (S18)

A second renderer on the same sim (GDD §07 R2: Experimental, a second renderer only). Guiding a villager is **always** first person (S18b: the F toggle is gone; the top-down guide is only the fallback when WebGL is missing, with a one-line toast, or with the dev flag `?fpv=0`). Earlier text: F switched between the top-down possession view and the villager's eyes; the last choice is remembered (browser storage), so possessing again returns to it. Off, nothing changes: the top-down map, banner, panels and zoom are untouched. The sim does not know it exists: `git diff --stat src/sim` shows no S18 line (the only sim-adjacent edit is that `renderer.js` exports `RenderView`). Esc: the browser frees the mouse first; the next Esc lets go of the villager (`game.fpvEscape` swallows an Esc that arrives in the same breath as the unlock).

**Technique: raw WebGL2, a per-chunk face mesher.** Chosen over a Canvas2D raycaster because the world is a 3D cell grid with hills, stairs, caves and tunnels at every level: a mesh of faces puts every level in the scene at once and the depth buffer sorts them, the cost is per face not per pixel (a software raycaster at 1280x720 is ~900k rays a frame), and the GPU does the lighting and fog. Zero dependencies, about 600 lines in `render/fpv.js`, inside the single bundled HTML.
- **Terrain** (`FpvRenderer.mesh`): for each 16 x 16 x D chunk within `VIEW_R` (28 cells) of the eye, one quad per face where a solid cell meets an open one (water: its top only, at 0.88), so a hill side, a stair, a ramp, a cave ceiling and a tunnel floor are all just faces; corners get ambient occlusion; each face carries its sky flag (no natural ground within four cells above the open cell, the same test as `light.js underground()`). Unseen cells are baked dark (an unseen surface dim and bluish like the top-down map's unexplored land, unseen rock nearly black). Per-face brightness jitter, a pixel-noise and a cell-edge line in the shader give the voxel look with no texture.
- **Cache and change tracking** (UI layer, no sim hook): a chunk's mesh is rebuilt when a signature (a hash of its cells plus the count of cells seen in it, combined with its four neighbours') changes. The signatures of the chunks in range are recomputed at most 5 times a second, or at once when the eye enters a new chunk; at most 6 meshes are built a frame, nearest first; meshes outside range are freed.
- **No sim chunks are made.** Cells are read through the renderer's own `RenderView` (resident chunks as they are, anything else regrown in the renderer's bounded LRU). The smoke test stands a villager 150 cells out on unexplored land and checks the sim's generation count, resident set and cache are unchanged while 25 chunk meshes draw.
- **Billboards** (`render/sprites.js`, `spriteOf`): villagers (lineage hue and the lineage mark: dot / bar / triangle; child, asleep, ill, what they carry; no face when walking away), creatures (hopper, wolf, grazer, thornback), plants (tree, pine, bush with berries, tuft, sapling, stump, crops by stage), items by their `ITEMS` colour, furniture and stations (door, bed, hearth, kiln, forge, smoker, mound, bench, bellows, torch, lamp, shelf, vent, jar, rack), flames, graves, wagons, construction ghosts. Painted procedurally on 32 x 32 canvases into a 512 x 512 atlas the first time a key is wanted, upright quads with an alpha cutout and a soft ground shadow, nearest-neighbour. Same palette as the map, so a slime is the same slime. Only where somebody has looked (seen cells), within 24 cells, at most 1400.
- **Light**: `skyState(tick)` (pure): daylight ramps at dawn and dusk (the day is `season.js phase`), a golden hour, a night blue, a cooler winter and warmer summer from `warmthAt`, a sun and a moon crossing the sky, stars at night. Open faces get that colour; underground faces get only an ambient floor plus `lightSources(sim)` (torch 5, lamp 7, lit hearth/kiln/forge 4, the sim's radii) as warm point lights (the nearest 8, a flicker unless reduced flashing is on). Fog from 12 to 27 cells in the sky colour outdoors, to black underground. A cave looks dark beyond torchlight, a lit tunnel is a warm pool.
- **How it handles caves and levels**: there is no "viewed level" in first person. All z in range are meshed; the eye is at the villager's z + 0.74 and eases between cells (and between levels on stairs and ramps), faces seen through a cave mouth, from a ledge or up a shaft are simply in the depth buffer. Sky versus underground is per face (and per billboard), so the light changes at the mouth.

**Controls** (all in `ui/keys.js ACTIONS`, ctx `guide`, so remappable in Settings): `fpv` F toggle; `turnLeft` `,` and `turnRight` `.` (held; for when the mouse is not captured); `use` E or left click. The mouse look is the pointer (click the view to capture it; yaw free, pitch clamped to +-1.2 rad). WASD / arrows are `panUp/Left/Down/Right`, now relative to where you look: `fpvmath.walkDir(yaw, fwd, side)` turns the held keys into one cardinal cell step (the sim steps on axes only: `stepCell` zeroes dy when both are set), the dominant axis wins, a tie goes to forward/back. The faced cell for the torch (O) and support (U) keys follows the yaw (`ui.face` is set from it). The speed cap (2x) and the disabled skip buttons are unchanged (they key off possession).
- **Crosshair target** (`ui/fpvpick.js`, pure): a 3D DDA (`fpvmath.castCells`) finds the first solid cell within 7; the billboards the ray passes (sphere test) that are `usable` win when nearer. An entity is offered if the top-down could use it (tree, bearing bush or tuft, item, creature, furniture, site); rock is offered when it is `diggable` and within 3.2. The verb chip says what the sim's own `interact()` would do (Chop, Pick, Take, Strike, Dig, Build, Sleep...) or "Walk closer" / the blocker sentence.
- **Same commands as the top-down view** (`ui/usecmd.js`): `useFor(sim, v, {ent}|{cell})` -> `{tid}` or `{verb:"dig", x, y, z}` or why not; the top-down click (`targetOfPick`) and the crosshair both go through `game.useTarget`, so one target is one `pinput`, and a recorded possession replays identically whichever view was used (`tests/fpv.js` applies both to two sims and compares hashes). E in first person with nothing under the crosshair falls back to the cell you face (the top-down E).

**HUD** (DOM `#fpvhud`, classes `fv-*`, built for UI_SPEC §7 so S19 only re-skins it): the "Guiding <name>" plate with an Esc keycap and a lock-badged `2x` chip when the speed is capped; hazard icons (bad air, cave-in, dark) from `hazardLines`; a crosshair; the verb chip (icon + E keycap + verb + what) hung over the target; need rings (food, water, energy, warmth, health, and fear only when above 0) with an icon in each, the ring stroke carries the level and an icon plus pulse a critical one; the held item sprite (`sprites.item`) with a put-down keycap; the blocker line; a vignette; a screen-edge tint for hurt and for a critical need. With Settings > reduced flashing the edge and rings do not pulse or flash (a steady frame instead) and the torchlight does not flicker. The side panels, minimap and the top-down banner are hidden while it is on; the top bar and toasts stay.

**Performance.** Per frame: signatures at most 5 x a second, at most 6 chunk meshes, billboards within 24 cells, 8 lights, one draw call per chunk plus three small dynamic ones; bounded by `VIEW_R` whatever the size of the world. ## Clock rebase (6.18b, S18b, D35)

**What changed.** The calendar day is **12000 ticks = 10 real minutes at 1x** (was 1800 = 90 s; this supersedes K2's 90 s day and the 4-year life in the GDD Units and clock box). A season is 25 days (4.2 h), a year 100 days (16.7 h), a life **5 game years** (`LIFE_DAYS` 500). Night is 30% of the day (3 real minutes). The calendar day is one constant, `DAY` in `src/sim/clock.js` (the test guard overrides it with `SLIME_DAY`; the game never does).

**The rule (author direction, revised the same day).** Only the **calendar** (day and night, season, year, the date) and **age** are counted in calendar days. **Every other duration and rate is flat ticks** with the real-time feel it had on the old day, written against `DUR_DAY` = 1800 (`clock.js`; 90 s at 1x; it is not the calendar day). `daily()` therefore runs every `DUR_DAY` ticks. Only the energy and food balance is re-tuned. Needs: `villager.js NEED_DAY` = 12000 (hunger 0.25, thirst 0.35, energy 0.7 awake / 2.0 asleep in a bed, per 12000 ticks: a ration about every 14 min at 1x, a night's sleep fills the energy; a sleep begun at night lasts the night).

**Constants changed (old -> new, and why).**

| Constant | Old -> new | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `DAY` (clock.js) | 1800 -> 12000 | author: 10 real minutes per game day |
| `DUR_DAY` (new) | - -> 1800 | the unit every flat duration is written in (fuel, regrowth, spoilage, illness, cooldowns, `daily()` period) |
| `LIFE_DAYS` | 400 -> 500 | 5 game years |
| hunger, thirst, energy per tick | per 1800 ticks -> per `NEED_DAY` (12000) | needs follow the calendar so a winter (25 days) and a night fit; flat, not tied to `DAY` |
| cold drain and cold harm | per 1800 -> per `NEED_DAY` | cold is the season: a winter is 25 calendar days |
| mood, healing, harm of hunger, thirst, illness, ageing | unchanged per 1800 | flat durations |
| `FRAIL_DAYS` (ageing damage) | 150 -> 100 | `tests/lifespan.js`: the old-age death window centres on 4.95 years (range 3.9-6.2); independent of `DAY` |
| plant lifespans (`LIFE`) | days of 1800 ticks -> calendar days (`ageOf` uses `DAY`) | they are ages and must outlast a calendar winter (a bush died every winter otherwise) |
| `BREED_P` | 0.025 per day -> 0.02 x `DUR_DAY` / `DAY` per daily call | breeding is tied to age: 0.02 per calendar day, spread over the daily calls |
| `BREED_COOLDOWN`, `SPLIT_COOLDOWN` | 45, 60 days -> 56, 75 calendar days (x `DAY`) | the 5-year life is 25% longer; without this the village tripled and starved in winter |
| skill `K` | 40 -> `181 * DAY / 12000` | D13: steady work reaches 0.7 after 30% of adult life (801 of 2667 work days at DAY 12000; `tests/skill_pacing.js`); follows `DAY` |
| `WORKDAY`, rested curve, `XP_PER_VALUE` | 1200 / 720 / 1 | unchanged (the work day is one `DUR_DAY`) |
| `FORAGE_SLOW` (tasks.js) | - -> 24 | K3: berry, tuber, root and harvest actions take 24x longer. With a ration per 12000 ticks and a 17-tick pick, food was 1-5% of labour; now `tests/k3.js` reads about 55% (30-70% enforced) |
| `Wander` | unbounded -> tethered to 30 cells of the camp | on a long day leisure carried villagers 300 cells off and they died of thirst (found while tuning) |
| `jobs.js` sleep commitment | to energy 0.75 -> to 0.98 while it is night | one sleep a night, not naps broken by the first chore (anchor 2) |
| `EXP_TIME`, `WITNESS_EVERY`, fuel ticks, hearth cap | unchanged ticks | flat durations (named tick constants with real-time comments) |

**Fixtures** are regenerated (`rm tests/fixtures/*.json; node tools/make_fixtures.js`, about 45 s): `settled` is tick 36000 (20 x `DUR_DAY`, calendar day ~8: spring), `prewinter` / `midwinter` / `year2` follow the calendar (days 74, 87, 101). `tidy()` keeps eight villagers and 24 dried berries in the store, and clears near-misses and dead ends, so a fixture is a small fed village; the `fire` fixture also gets 30 dried berries.

**The 6.18 flake** (`localCount` crashing inside flora `sprout` during possession/chop; it passed on rerun). `localCount` reads `sim.ents.get(id)` for every id `ents.at()` returns, so a stale id (an id in the cell index with no entity) crashes it. `Entities.move()` re-indexed any entity it was handed, including one already removed (killed earlier in the same tick and still held by a loop), which leaves exactly such a stale id. Found by inspection, not reproduced; fixed (`move` ignores a removed entity) and covered by `tests/clock.js` section 5. It is not an endless-world cell-key problem (`cellIdx` is collision free).

**Fast-forward.** Next morning, Next season and MAX work unchanged (`skip.js` uses the calendar): from dusk the skip crosses the night in 3000 ticks, about 6 frame slices of 12 ms; a whole calendar day flat out takes about 0.1-0.5 s. Guiding a villager disables skips as before.

**Tests.** New: `tests/clock.js` (the clock, one adult over 2 days eats, drinks and sleeps once a night, Next morning, save round trip, the flake), `tests/lifespan.js`, `tests/skill_pacing.js`, `tests/k3.js` (prints the labour share), `tests/dayguard.js` (static: no test hard-codes 1800 or 12000; dynamic: the self-contained tests run under `SLIME_DAY=7500` and `20000`). ## UX stability (6.20)
From the user's playtest of 6.19b. `src/sim` is untouched. Five problems, each reproduced in the browser first, each with a `ui_smoke` check (`tools/s20.js`, also runnable alone with `node tools/ui_shots20.js [pointers|boxSelect|gauge|inspector|hints]`, which writes `tmp/ui-s20-*.png`).

## Core field and cave networks (7.20)
Phase 7 begins: the endless land gains **magic cores**, the **mana field** around them, and **cave networks** that replace the simple worms. Everything is a pure function of (seed, coordinates), like all terrain (S16). No dungeons, monsters or spawning yet (7.21-7.23). Decisions: DECISION_LOG D42 (OD-46..50 taken at their defaults).
- **Cores** (`sim/core.js`). One per 256-cell region by a hashed rule (about 85 regions in 100 roll one; it must stand on dry land, so about 60 in 100 hold one). A core is `{x, y, z, h, sh, spire}`; `sh` = shallow (within 7 of the ground). No region core within `SAFE_R` = 150 of the camp. The camp gets one extra **findable** core, `def.spawnCore`, 170-205 cells out in the first of eight directions that lands on dry ground: always shallow, always with a **crystal spire** (a plus of crystal, 5 high at the point) and a landmark kind `core` (`landmarks.js`, minimap letter `*`) so scouts and the minimap find it.
- **Mana** (`manaAt(def, x, y, z)`, `bandAt`). `mana = MANA_TAB[d]`, an **integer** lookup table of a quadratic (255 at the core, 0 at `MANA_R` = 84), over `d = oct(dx, dy) + 2 x |dz|` (depth counts double), the strongest core wins. **Bands** 0 wild, 1 stirring (14), 2 charged (60), 3 deep (130), 4 heart (200). No Math.exp / sin / random. Nothing within 66 cells of any camp has mana at any depth. `World.manaBand(x, y, z)` reads two per-chunk byte arrays genChunk now returns (`mb` = the band at the surface of each column, `md` = 8 below it) for the map tint.
- **Cave networks** (`sim/caves.js`). Cave regions of 96 cells: about 3 in 4 hold a **node** (a junction at depth 3..9; always one where a core stands). A region OWNS the tunnels that leave its node: east, south, sometimes south-east to the neighbouring nodes; 1-4 dead-end **spurs** (more near a core) that finish in a room; a **spur to the core** (ending in its chamber; the core cell `M.CORE` sits in the floor); and often an **entrance ramp** (straight, one level a step, out of a hillside). A tunnel is a straight line pushed sideways by a slow noise (tapered to nothing at its ends), made 4-connected, with a depth that slides between its ends plus a wander, kept under the ground (3 cells of cover) and at no more than **one level per step**, so every tunnel can be walked and the network spans many levels. Flat stretches are 3 wide, slopes 1. **Rooms** sit on flat stretches (r 3-5, bigger near a core); some are tall (6 high: chasms), some have a pool in the floor (underground lakes: water cells ringed by a walkable floor). All flagged `NATURAL` (its rock vault holds, the S12 rules). A tunnel strays at most about 205 cells east or south and 60 west or north of its region, so a chunk only asks the owners round it (`carveChunk`). Only tunnel steps and node data are cached (`def.rc`, bounded); the generator reads no other chunk.
- **The camp keeps its home caves** (`sim/worms.js`, D42 deviation a). Within `HOME_R` = 100 cells of the camp S16's worms stand exactly as they were and the networks do not carve; the land inside is byte-identical to before 7.20, so the early game and every fixture play as validated. ## Dungeons (7.21)
Most cores now sit in a **labyrinth**: a bounded, pure, order-independent maze of rooms, corridors, stairs and dead ends under the ground, with hidden traps, sealed caches and ancient workshops. No monsters or spawning (7.22) and no delve expedition or core-chamber choice (7.23). Decisions: DECISION_LOG D43 (D42's defaults: procedural rooms plus a few authored set pieces, iron-only slow walls, core shards an ordinary material).
- **Generator** (`sim/dungeon.js`; `dungeonOf(def, core)`, cached per def in `def.dc`, bounded). The footprint is a grid of 8-cell modules, 6-10 a side (**48-80 cells across**), the core's module placed by a hash so the core can be off-centre. **2-5 levels**, three cells apart (stand `s`, air `s`, `s+1`, a one-cell slab at `s+2`; the core's level is the core's z, others are +-3). A module is dug on a level only where the lowest ground over it leaves two cells of cover over the slab and the land is dry (>= 12); only what the core's module reaches (neighbours on its level, stairs between levels) is kept, so footprints are ragged. Per level a randomised Kruskal spanning tree over the dug modules plus ~9% extra edges (loops) is cut as **one-cell L corridors** between room anchors (so leaves are dead ends; some corridors sprout blind stubs); **stairs** join levels (a stair module is a fixed 6 x 4 room on both levels, a 3-step stair: stand s, s+1, s+2, then the upper floor; the steps are real solid cells so the pathfinder's own rules climb them). Rooms are 3-6 cells or one of four authored **set pieces** (`SETPIECES`: pillared hall, ossuary, shrine, gauntlet: a picture of the floor with `#` pillars, `c` caches, `t` trap spots). **Room kinds** (`ROOM_KINDS`): hall, trap hall, vault (treasure), den (the 7.22 spawn rooms), ancient workshop (1-2 per dungeon), and the core chamber (6 x 6, the core cell `M.CORE` at its middle); dead ends are mostly vaults and trap halls, rooms near the core more often vaults and traps. About 25 caches and 40 traps per dungeon.
- **Entrances.** Up to two **links**: a `caves.js` tunnel from the core's cave node to a door cell on the footprint edge (the side facing the node first; a tunnel that clears the walls, validated end to end); a core with a dungeon swaps its 7.20 spur-to-the-core for these (the `ownedBy` hook is `def.dunOf(core)`, set in `baseDef`, so `caves.js` need not import `dungeon.js`). And a **ruin**: a straight stair from a door up to the surface (always when no link was possible, otherwise 5 times in 12); its mouth is a landmark of kind `ruin` (`landmarks.js`) between two stone posts. No entrance, no dungeon (the core stays plain, as in 7.20). Inside its footprint the labyrinth wins over a natural cave (`carveDungeon`), so the pure layout and the real cells agree exactly (tested); nothing within 100 cells of a camp changes.
- **Walls and cache** (append-only materials `DWALL` 34 "ancient stonework", `CACHE` 35 "sealed cache"; `F.DUNGEON` 128 on the air, with `F.NATURAL`). Every solid cell touching the air (walls, floors, ceilings) is `DWALL`: `DIG` row needs an **iron pick** and takes 1920 ticks (**1/6 the speed of hard rock**, 320). A cache digs open without a tool in 240 ticks and spills its loot (`traps.js openCache` -> `lootAt`). `light.js` counts both as natural ground (a labyrinth is dark: torches).
- **Traps** (`sim/traps.js`, rows `TRAPS` = label, damage, parts, hardness; kinds `dart`, `pit`, `collapse`, `gas`; the pure placement is `dungeon.js` `trapAt`/`trapList`; pits only in rooms with open floor under them, others on straight corridor stretches or trap halls). **Hidden:** each tick a villager is inside a labyrinth (sim.js calls `trapStep` for cells with `F.DUNGEON`) every unspotted trap within `R` cells on their level (R = sight - 1, 1..3; a torch widens it) is spotted with chance `0.045 x intelligence^2 + 0.05 x craft level`. (No `perception` trait exists yet: intelligence and craft skill stand in.) **Seen** traps are listed in `sim.trp.seen` and their cell gets `F.OBSTACLE`, so every path avoids them (in a one-cell corridor that blocks it until disarmed); the map draws a warning glyph with a one-word label (Darts, Pit, Collapse, Gas) in the angled view, the flat fallback and first person (a billboard), and the cell card says "Trap: Darts, spotted." Whoever steps on an **unseen** one sets it off: dart 25 hit points, pit 20 and a drop one level through the floor, collapse 40 and rubble in the passage, gas 12 and a cloud (`sim.trp.gas`, 600 ticks, counted as bad air by `underground.badAt`). **Disarm** is the order verb `disarm` (right-click a marked plate with villagers selected; `{type:"order", verb:"disarm", x, y, z}`, craft skill, `genDisarm`, tasks verb `disarm`): chance `0.55 + 0.4 x craft level + 0.3 x (intelligence - 1) - hardness`; success leaves `trap_parts`, a slip sets the trap off on them. `lair.restricted` returns **`dungeon`** for every `F.DUNGEON` cell, so autonomous villagers never wander in or take jobs there; `plan` lets only `disarm` through (everything else needs possession).
- **Treasure** (`LOOT` table in `dungeon.js`; `lootAt(def, x, y, z)` is pure): copper ingots, gems, crystal, **core shards** (ordinary item), ancient tools, stone tablets, relics (named in the chronicle: "the Pale Crown"), core-touched ore; the rows open up by mana band (band >= 1 shards and tools, 2 tablets and relics, 3 core ore) and the rolls rise with it (1 + one at band 2 + one at band 4, counts +1 at band 3+), so the mean value of a cache near the core is about 2.5 x one far from it (tested). Items: `core_shard`, `ancient_tool`, `tablet`, `relic`, `trap_parts` (shown in the stock bars once held).
- **Ancient workshops.** A workshop room carries a craft (from the discovery set); a villager standing in one adds it to `sim.trp.ws` and a Curiosity (an itch to tinker) for that craft, and `knowledge.evidenceOf` returns 0.6 for it, which is the rediscovery discount (R11).
- **Fog.** Nothing special: the labyrinth is under rock, `revealDisc` marks only the surface and the faces open to the air, and walls stop the look rays, so it is dark and unknown until somebody goes in (test: the whole footprint seen from the surface reveals no labyrinth cell). A map glyph exists only for a **spotted** trap; unspotted ones draw nothing.
- **Saves.** `SAVE_VERSION` 13; `sim.trp = {n, seen, done, gas, ws}` is saved and hashed (additive: a v12 save loads with an empty ledger and its fixtures are unchanged, since nothing a camp's village could have touched in its first seasons moved).
- **Tests.** `tests/dungeon.js` (quick set): the generator called directly (determinism from a cold cache over 89 dungeons, 48-80 across, 2-5 levels, entrances, set pieces, all trap kinds, no random or clock in the source), every cache / trap / room reachable from an entrance (the generator's own walk) and walked again in the real cells with the pathfinder's step rules, chunk generation order independence, nothing within 100 cells of 6 camps, fog, the ruin landmark, loot by distance and band, wall cost; the `trap` fixture (`tools/make_fixtures.js` `prepTrap`: two villagers each two cells from a hidden trap in a labyrinth): spotting, the path avoiding it, a disarm order (defused or set off), a master craftsman defusing it, an unseen trap going off by kind, autonomy refusing the cells, an ancient workshop as a Curiosity and evidence, the save round trip. `tools/ui_smoke_p7.js` (run it AFTER `ui_smoke.js`; the 7.20 cores check moved there): spotting in the page, the cell card, right-click -> Disarm, first person, the flat canvas fallback; screenshots `tmp/ui-721-dungeon-slice.png`, `-overview.png`, `-fpv.png`, `-flat.png`.
- **Seams for 7.22 / 7.23.** `dun.rooms` lists every room with `kind` (`den` = a spawn point for 7.22; `core` = the guardian's chamber; `workshop`; `vault`), its floor rectangle, level `j` and `d` (distance to the core, in modules and levels); `roomAt(dun, x, y, z)` and `dungeonAt(def, x, y)` answer for a cell; `bandAt` gives the danger there; the door cells are `dun.doors` / `dun.entrances` (a delve expedition goes in at an entrance, `reachable(dun)` is its map); `F.DUNGEON` cells are what a monster's habitat reads; `sim.trp.seen` is "the trap blocking them" for a stall summary; `sim.trp.ws` and the `relic` / `tablet` / `ancient_tool` items are where heirloom history, ancient designs and knowledge tablets (R10) attach; the core chamber's `M.CORE` still has no dig row (7.23 decides harvest or leave). Not done: a dungeon outline drawn as it is explored, danger pips on the minimap, perception as a trait, trap rows as a data file the player can read (they are in code), the effect of a tablet or a blueprint fragment, a lit-by-default core chamber.

## Monsters and spawning (7.22)
The mana of a core now breeds monsters in the dark. They are ordinary `fauna` entities (rows spread into `FAUNA`), so fear, the one melee swing, the inspector, possession and the renderers treat them like the thornback. Nothing here runs within 66 cells of a camp (no mana), so a camp's history and its fixtures are unchanged.
- **Roster** (`sim/monsters.js`, rows `MONSTERS`; habitat weights `w` by mana band 0..4, spawn point kinds `where`, `drops`, `scare` = how much fear it causes (1 = the thornback), `fearDmg`, `aggro`, `leash`, `patrol`): **cave crawler** (16 HP, 5 a bite, swarms of 2-4, band 1+, raids, `dark`), **coreback** (110 HP, 30 a bite, band 2+, retreats at 30%, ignores light), **spitter** (26 HP, 9 a shot from up to 5 cells, backs away from anyone within 2, band 2+, `dark`), **core guardian** (320 HP, 40 a blow, `boss`). Drops are items: `chitin`, `core_hide`, `venom_sac`, `core_shard` (new item rows).
- **Spawner** (`spawnPass`, run by `faunaTick` when `tick % 100 === 55`). World simulation, not a wave timer: it walks the ACTIVE chunks only. A chunk offers pure spawn points (`chunkInfo(def, cx, cy)`, cached per chunk): `den` (dungeon rooms of kind den), `cave` (dry cave chambers, `caves.js chambersIn`), `surface` (one hashed ground column per chunk per pass, only where the ground is band >= 2; the rate is 1/8 by day) and `boss` (the core chamber). A point rolls `spawnRate(mana, kind, night)` per 10000 (zero below band 1, monotone in mana; dens 1.5x caves) from a HASH of (seed, point, period): no random stream, so nothing else reshuffles. A spawn needs darkness (no torch, lamp or fire within its radius + 3: `lightBlocks`), nobody within 6 cells, an underground cell (surface points: open air) and room under `regionCap(mana)` (2 + mana/32, per 64 x 64 region by the monster's home) and under `MAX` = 90 live monsters. Surface spawns burn off by day.
- **Guardian.** One per core that has a labyrinth, at its chamber, spawned when its chunk is active; a slain one is remembered in `sim.wild.gk` (core keys) and never returns. Drops 3-5 `core_shard`. 7.23 builds the harvest-or-leave choice and the deeds on this.
- **Budget.** A chunk that unloads (`chunks.js unload`) or falls asleep (`dormant.js`) loses its monsters (they are re-rolled when it wakes); `MAX` bounds the rest.
- **Behaviour** (`monster`). Patrol round home; hunt a villager within `aggro` and leash to `leash`; a **crawler raids**: from a home within 40 cells of the camp it sees 14 cells farther (`st` "raid"). A monster walks greedily and is stopped by what stops a creature: **walls**, **doors** (a door cell is closed to monsters), **light** (`dark` rows will not step into a lit cell) and **mana** (OD-49: it never steps onto a cell below band 1; `e.hr`, a home radius, is the override a hand-placed monster carries, as in the fixture).
- **Village pressure.** The `danger` Concern gains a monster line (within 30 cells of camp: level 2, level 3 when they raid or someone is afraid; "Walls, doors and light keep them out; spear crews can clear them (Clear order)"). Villagers flee by the thornback's rules; `scare` scales the fear a creature causes (the strongest in sight counts, identical numbers for rows with 1), and a villager with a **copper spear in hand fears a weak thing (`scare` < 0.5) a fifth as much** so a spear crew can clear a den. The new **Clear** order verb (`order` verb `clear`, Orders dock, right-click a monster) sends spear-carrying villagers at the monsters round the target (area 10, never the guardian; it needs a spear, blocker `NO_MATERIAL` otherwise, and it is let into a labyrinth like Disarm). Possession is the way to fight a coreback or the guardian.
- **Fights read.** Billboards in `render/sprites.js` (oblique map and first person, one painter), flat shapes in the canvas2D fallback (`renderer.js`), the hit ring and "-N" / "miss" on whoever is struck (existing), a red slash (melee) or green streak (spit) from the attacker (`e.sh = [x, y, z, tick, melee]`), "Crawler: Biting" tags over monsters that are fighting, the first-person chip "Attack Cave crawler" (verb `strike` now reads "Attack"), a "dodged" pop when a blow misses the guided villager.
- **Saves.** `SAVE_VERSION` 14 (additive fields `mon`, `ck`, `sf`, `hr`, `sh` on fauna, `sim.wild.gk`, three item rows): a v13 save loads as it is; no fixture was regenerated (one is new: `monsters`).
- **Tests.** `tests/monsters.js` (quick): the rules are pure (rate monotone in mana, caps rise, light blocks, surface needs band 2, complete rows); no surface spawn without a core within 84 or inside 66 of a camp over 6 seeds; the spawner in an active labyrinth (caps per region, OD-49, deterministic, torches on every point cut the spawns to 0, a sleeping chunk spawns nothing); a camp with no mana spawns nothing and touches no stream; the `monsters` fixture (Concern at once, villagers flee, a raid, light and a door as defences, the Clear order, a ranged shot); the guardian (band 4, once, drops, save); determinism and a mid-raid save; memory on a four-leg walk. The browser checks are in `tools/ui_smoke_p7.js`.
- **Seams for 7.23.** `sim.wild.gk` (slain guardians by core key; harvest or leave), `e.ck` on the guardian, `MONSTERS.guardian.drops`, `spawnPass` skips a core only through `gk`, `chunkInfo(...).boss` = the chamber cells, the Clear order's `c.clr` flag in `jobs.js` (a delve crew would reuse the dungeon allowance), `monsterMay` / `bandHere` for danger pips, and the raid state `st: "raid"` for a delve stall summary.

## Delving gameplay and the core itself (7.23)
The last stage of Phase 7: a party can be sent into a labyrinth to map it, a slain guardian puts the core in the player's hands, and the map says where the danger is. Decisions: DECISION_LOG D45 (OD-47: a harvested core is gone for good).
- **Delve expedition** (`sim/delve.js`, `kind: "delve"` on the S8/S17 expedition record, so the crew link, the Recall button, the stall summary and the report card all carry over). `{type:"expedition", op:"start", kind:"delve", ids | crew, core?}`: `core` is the core's key `"x,y"` (else the labyrinth nearest the first member); a target that has no labyrinth is refused `NO_DUNGEON`. It searches nothing: every 20 ticks it gives each idle member the ORDERS the player could give by hand, one at a time: **scout** to the ruin's mouth, **move** down to the door, then inside, by steps along the floor (`walkDist`, a BFS under the pathfinder's own rules): **disarm** a spotted trap within 20 steps, else **move** beside a seen sealed cache (they work it open in `CACHE_TICKS` = 120 ticks, the loot packed into `x.loot`), else **move** to the nearest unseen room (the core chamber only once its guardian is dead: a delve party does not fight; possession is the way). Loose finds beside a member (trap parts, a monster's drop) are swept into the pack. A member who has not moved for `STUCK_TICKS` = 300 is stalled; twice that and the target is given up (`x.skip`). Rooms count as mapped when a member stands in one. A party that is idle for 100 ticks, all inside, is done ("mapped").
- **Recall rules** suit the danger: any member under **60%** health (the plain expedition says 50%), the party's **brands** burning low (`DELVE_LIGHT` = 6000 ticks, counted only while somebody is inside; `v.br` widens sight to `LIT_SIGHT` in `light.js sightAt`, so a party sees and spots traps by its own light), hunger under 30%, `DELVE_TIME` = 14000 ticks in all, the labyrinth mapped, or the Recall button. In "home" they `scout` back to the camp and the haul is unpacked into the stockpile (`finishDelve`; a cache is packed straight into the party's pack, so it arrives with them and is lost with them), with a report in the chronicle and on the card. `delving(sim, v)` lets a delve party through `lair.restricted`'s `dungeon` (jobs.js, like Disarm and Clear); autonomy still never goes there.
- **Stall summary** names the thing in the way: `x.stall` is `TRAP:<kind>`, `MONSTER:<species>` or `NO_PATH` (a stalled member's nearest spotted trap within 6 cells, else the nearest monster within 9; a frightened member counts); `ui/text.js delveStallText` says "a darts trap is in the way" / "a coreback is in the way", and `crewSummary` and the Expedition card use it.
- **The core chamber** (`sim/cm.js`; `sim.cm = {n, pend, left, gone, known}`, saved and hashed). When a guardian dies (by a guided villager or anybody: `monsterDrops(sim, tgt, att)` -> `guardianSlain`) the chronicle records "X, guided, slew the guardian of the north-east ridge" and the core key goes into `pend`; the card in `ui/corechoice.js` (`#corechoice`, in the map view and first person) offers **Harvest** or **Leave** and nothing else answers it. `{type:"core", op:"harvest"|"leave", key}` (`NO_TARGET` when nothing is pending for that key). **Harvest**: `HARVEST_SHARDS` = 24 core shards into the stockpile (a guardian drops 3-5), the core cell `M.CORE` becomes air (gone for good: OD-47), the chronicle says "The core of the north-east ridge was harvested", and the mana field **fades over `FADE_TICKS` = 12000 ticks** (a flat tick duration). **Leave**: the core stands, nothing changes, final (a core is only offered once).
- **How the fade works** (`core.js`): `fadeMana(m, age)` is a pure integer line (m at age 0, 0 at FADE_TICKS). The harvest ledger lives on the SIM: `sim.def = ownDef(world.def)` is the sim's own view of the (shared, memoised) world definition, carrying `gone` (core key -> harvest tick) and `fb` (the clock box `fb.now`, set each step). `coresNear` returns a harvested core as a view carrying `gt` and `fb`, so `manaAt`, `bandAt`, `spawnRate` inputs, the region cap, `lootAt` and the danger pip all fall together; **terrain generation reads the raw field** (`manaOf(..., raw)`), because the rock is regrown from the seed and cannot depend on a game clock: ore already in the stone stays, and so does the map tint (the per-chunk `mb`/`md` bytes). `applyCm` rebuilds the ledger on load. Spawn points are recomputed against the live field (`monsters.js spawnPass`).
- **Map readability.** The **minimap** draws danger pips (a coloured dot by mana band 1..4 at each seen core or ruin landmark, a red dot for every monster standing in a seen cell, bigger for a guardian) and the **dungeon outline as it is explored** (violet pixels for the seen floor cells of every labyrinth in `sim.cm.known`, the ones somebody has stood in). The angled map and the flat fallback draw the same explored cells as a violet outline and faint wash on the level being looked at (`mapdecor.js drawDungeonOutline`, called from `drawTraps`). **Trap glyphs** (existing, checked): a warning triangle in the kind's colour with a one-word label; the inside is now a different mark per kind (exclamation, pit hole, slash pair, bubbles) so colour is not the only cue.
- **First person.** The two 7.22 screenshot problems: (1) billboards were sized `1.0` cells for a crawler, filling the bottom third of the view when one stood beside you; sizes now live in `sprites.js FAUNA_SIZE` (crawler 0.5, spitter 0.62, coreback 1.3, guardian 1.9, a slime 0.9). (2) The empty dark chip at the foot of the HUD was `.fv-held` (the "in hand" chip) shown with nothing in hand; it is hidden unless something is held.
- **Chronicle deeds:** "X was the first to enter a labyrinth" (`trapStep`), "X, guided, slew the guardian of the north-east ridge", "The core of the north-east ridge was harvested" / "... was left as it is". A core is named by its compass side of the camp (`coreName`).
- **Saves.** `SAVE_VERSION` 15 (additive: `sim.cm`, `kind` and the delve fields on an expedition, `br` on a member); a v14 save loads as it is. No existing fixture was regenerated; three new ones were derived from `settled`.
- **Fixtures** (`tools/make_fixtures.js`, derived, no extra run): `delve` (three villagers on the nearest labyrinth's first door), `guardian` (a slime holding a copper spear in the core chamber, the guardian two cells away with 15 hit points), `coreslain` (the guardian slain, the choice pending).
- **Tests.** `tests/delve.js` (quick set, a few seconds): the fade as a pure integer line, labels, no random in the new files; the guardian kill by a guided slime (the deed, the choice waiting); Leave (nothing changes, final); Harvest (shards, cell open, the field read through the clock box at 0, half and the full duration: bands, spawn rate, region cap and cache richness all fall, a second sim of the same seed is untouched, terrain unchanged); the save round trip mid-fade; the delve (maps rooms, clears a trap, opens a cache, packs loot, burns light only inside, recall on wounds and on light, the haul in the stockpile, a coreback named in the stall summary, the Delve offer in the panel, a save mid-delve); billboard sizes. Browser checks are in `tools/ui_smoke_p7.js` (22 checks in all; the 7.23 ones click Harvest and Leave, start a delve from the panel, read the outline on the map and minimap, and see a seen monster as a danger pip). Screenshots: `tmp/ui-723-core-choice.png`, `tmp/ui-723-dungeon-outline.png`, `tmp/ui-723-fpv-scale.png`.
- **Seams (beyond Phase 7).** `x.skip` / `x.vis` are the party's map (a "known rooms" overlay could read them); the choice card is a generic `sim.cm.pend` list; Leave is final but a later "return to the core" order could reopen it; the field fades for spawns and loot but not for ore in stone or the map tint (a regrown chunk cannot read a clock); a delve party carries no spear and never fights (a "delve with spears" variant would reuse the Clear order's `c.clr` flag); cave-only labyrinths (no surface ruin) are reachable only by a party already inside (cave-network pathing from the surface is bounded by the path budget); room mapping is by standing in a room (a torch-lit look would map faster). Not done: heirloom history for relics, blueprint fragments and tablet effects (R10), a perception trait, magic phenomena from core shards (OD-46).
