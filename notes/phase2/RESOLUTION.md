# Phase 2 resolution — director rulings (Opus)

Inputs: notes/phase1/{A,B,C,D}*.md, notes/phase2/design_challenges_tech.md, notes/phase2/tech_challenges_design.md.
These rulings are CANON for the GDD drafters. "DEFAULT" = recommended, revisit after the named prototype. Every number here is a starting value to be tuned, not a promise.

## The organising sentence
**Autonomy maintains; the player advances.** Villagers keep themselves alive, fed, sheltered, repaired and supplied without being asked. Whatever is *new* — a new kind of building, a new place, a new direction of research, a new scale of operation — comes from the player (by order, blueprint, standing order, possession or priority). This is what lets the village survive a long expedition and still leaves the player something to do.

## Rulings

| # | Topic | Ruling | Status | Validate with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Pacing / lifespan | A villager lives **~10 real hours at 1× speed** (tunable 8–12). Game year ≈ 1/4 of a life. All skill-rate, transmission and genetics numbers are derived from this anchor, not set independently. Speeds 1×–5× plus pause. | DEFAULT | Prototype "lifespan vs craft transmission" |
| R2 | Camera | **One camera**: fixed-pitch 3/4 oblique view onto a z-sliced world (current level + dimmed level below, roof/wall cutaway, ghost of level above). All sprites drawn 3/4. Possession uses the same camera with tighter follow and direct movement keys. First-person, Delver billboard 3D, DQ XI dual-view and side-view are **Experimental / post-MVP**, and only via a second renderer on the same sim. | DEFAULT | Prototype "3/4 slice readability"; billboard stub later |
| R3 | World representation | Data model = **3D integer cell grid with z-levels** (DF-like), chunked 16³, ~2 bytes/cell, terrain destructible. Rendered as slices, not as voxels. The GDD must present the evaluated alternatives (layered-2D, side-view 2D, billboard 2.5D, full voxel). MVP map ≈ 128×128×24. | DEFAULT | Prototype "layered-2D vs 3D grid on the slice" |
| R4 | Population | Designed for **6–60 named villagers per settlement**, sim ceiling ~100. Larger operations are handled by crews/standing orders, not by more individuals. | DECIDED | Perf prototype |
| R5 | Job board | One board, one score function. Explanation classes in order: **critical need > player order > standing order > village demand > personal leisure**. Needs override orders only on a continuous survival curve when critical ("a starving village told to build still eats first"). Orders bypass personal temper/preference. Every rejection emits a machine-readable **blocker** (no tool, no path, no material, no skill, reserved, unsafe…) that drives the "Why?" panel. Orders reserve work but don't freeze adjacent urgent jobs. | DECIDED | Prototype "order/need thrash" + "Why panel truthful" |
| R6 | Crews | A crew = named selection of villagers + a default task + a stall summary. No extra scoring layer. "Five on lumber" and an expedition party are the same object. | DECIDED | — |
| R7 | Standing orders | "Keep N of X" = stock target with a hysteresis band (refill below ~80%, stop at 100%), shown as a stock bar. Every gather order can be flipped to "keep N" in one click. Auto-promotion suggestions ("you've ordered this 3×") are post-MVP QoL. | DECIDED | — |
| R8 | Autonomy band | Autonomous without orders: eating, sleeping, hauling, repair, replanting, firewood, healing, fleeing, basic shelter from the **starter shelters** (leaf bed, lean-to) and any player blueprint the player has flagged **"auto-build"**. New functional building kinds come only from the player's designs. A "Steward" (autonomously choosing from the blueprint library) is a post-MVP option. | DECIDED | Prototype "unattended 2-hour survival ≥90%" |
| R9 | Possession | Same Action/Controller pipeline as AI (C). **Divine inspiration** = +10% action speed, +15% XP, and **Steadfast**: the possessed villager does not panic, flee or break under fear. No yield/damage/need bonus. Needs keep running; control is released only on collapse (incapacitation), never on a warning. No timer, no cooldown. Death while possessed is ordinary permadeath. Rationale: the author wants a felt "extra push… defeat a difficult enemy"; Steadfast delivers that qualitatively without making possession the optimal way to work. | DEFAULT | Prototype "possession feel / buff perceptible" + bot band (possessed output ≤1.25× delegated) |
| R10 | Knowledge model | **MVP: single layer.** Each villager holds per-craft *familiarity* (0–1) and skill XP; a craft is "known" by a villager when familiarity > 0. Capability = "a living villager who knows it" checked when a job is picked. B's notion/know-that layer is merged into this. Knowledge spreads by working beside / watching (slow), and by a **Teach** action (fast, costs the teacher's time). Documents/libraries are a later capability that preserves familiarity but caps skill at ~0.3–0.4. | DECIDED for MVP | Prototype "skill-only vs notion model" if stories feel thin |
| R11 | Knowledge loss | If the last holder dies, the craft is unavailable — but **evidence** (built kilns, stored products, ruins) makes rediscovery cheap: a tinker job near evidence rediscovers at a large discount. No permanent locks, no silent downgrade. | DECIDED | Prototype "forget/recover loop" |
| R12 | Discovery | Data-defined **phenomena**: rules that fire when material properties meet a context (heat, moisture, enclosure, time). Discovery via **witnessing** (villager sees a phenomenon happen: salience × attention × intelligence × pressure) and **tinkering** (idle/assigned villager experiments with materials within reach; consumes material; records dead ends). The 2-season "accident" timer is replaced by **systemic world events** that naturally create preconditions (lightning fires, erosion/cave-ins exposing ore, seasonal drought) plus a **per-seed solvability validator** at worldgen. Player sees an Almanac (known crafts, holders, dead ends) and **Curiosities** (observed-but-unexplained things) with near-miss hints that never name the product. | DECIDED | Prototype "slice discovery chain: no soft-lock" |
| R13 | Tech paths | One discovery system, **one path on the first planet** (craft/engineering), with **alternate implementations** for the same capability slot (e.g. preserve food by salting / drying / smoking / cold cellar) chosen by environment. Biotech-like options exist as alternates using husbandry & genetics. **Collective belief: Experimental, deferred** (rules kept in OPEN_DECISIONS). Mysticism folds into culture. | DECIDED | — |
| R14 | Eras | Eras are **labels derived from capabilities held** (e.g. "Hearth age", "Clay age", "Copper age"), shown in the chronicle. Never gates. slime-village's quest chain becomes chronicle "firsts", not unlocks. | DECIDED | — |
| R15 | Species | **Slimes only** for the first planet. Species is a data table (traits, needs, body plan) so another species can be added later. Dorfs are a candidate future species. | DECIDED | — |
| R16 | Genetics | Keep slime-village `settle()` soft cap and mixture inheritance. Add **convex trait upkeep** (high traits cost food/sleep), **environment-dependent value** (cold-hardy is useless in the jungle), **clone limit** (solo split ≤1 per life, clones regress toward mean). Genes move output ±25%; tools (1.5–5×) and skills (≤1.8×) carry the large multipliers. Hidden genotype / inbreeding depression added only if the breed-best bot beats the gate. | DEFAULT | Bot: breed-best colony ≤1.15× a normal one after 30 generations |
| R17 | Skills | XP from real output, challenge-matched; player-placed blocks give zero XP when re-mined; per-day diminishing ("rested") curve; ceiling bounded by genetic potential. **Skills gate quality and speed, never the ability to attempt** (no permadeath soft-lock). Profession = label derived from top skill. | DECIDED | Grind-bot |
| R18 | Rooms & buildings | **Hybrid**: flood-fill detects enclosed rooms (natural overhead rock counts as roof); player assigns a purpose; the room checks requirements (furniture/stations/space/light/temperature) and shows a checklist. Quality is graded, not binary; oversize lowers quality rather than failing. A "building" = one or more rooms + optional purpose. | DECIDED | Prototype "heat/enclosure context" |
| R19 | Blueprints | Capture a region (cells + furniture) → saved blueprint with material classes (any wood / any stone) and rotation. Place as ghost; "stamp ×N" for "build 10 houses". Build order: supports first. Partially built is a valid, visible state. | DECIDED | — |
| R20 | Hazards & combat | MVP: **cold** (shelter/insulation matters), **cave-in** (unsupported spans in mines), **bad air** (pocket tag), **one wounding fauna**. Full combat system deferred. Possessed fights use the same melee action as AI. | DECIDED | — |
| R21 | Logistics | Hauling is baseline behaviour. The player's lever is the **transport tier** (hand → basket/box → backpack → wagon → animal cart → rail) and stockpile placement. No belts, no drones (D3). No priority matrix in MVP. | DECIDED | — |
| R22 | Time away | The world simulates while the game runs (may run unfocused/backgrounded). **No offline catch-up in MVP.** Far settlements (later) use aggregate LOD. On return the player gets a **chronicle digest** of what happened. | DEFAULT | Open decision: offline progression |
| R23 | Determinism | Fixed tick, named counter-based RNG streams, integer positions/timers, command log, periodic state hash, strict sim/render split. Transcendental math lint-restricted in sim, not rewritten. No netcode now; first multiplayer form is **async snapshot visiting**. | DECIDED | Save round-trip + replay test |
| R24 | Engine | **Not chosen.** The GDD lists criteria and candidates only. slime-village (JS) is reused for **patterns, tests and data tables**, not as an engine. | DECIDED | — |
| R25 | Testing as design | The headless harness + **exploit bots** (grind, breed-best, possess-swap, max-expansion, absent-player, over-ordering) are an MVP feature, extending slime-village's headless/sweep/save-roundtrip approach. | DECIDED | — |
| R26 | Scope estimate | D's estimate stands as an ASSUMPTION: MVP 7–12 FTE-months; full first planet 30–60. Possession's real floor is 3.5–6.5 months (tech review); keep its MVP minimal. | NOTE | — |
| R27 | Exploration → discovery | Exploration feeds discovery directly: new materials, new fauna/flora, ruins and phenomena seen in the wild enter Curiosities. Mining uses **area designation** ("dig this region", "follow this vein", "seek copper") — never per-block clicking. | DECIDED | — |

## Cuts / deferrals (from both reviews)
- Cut from MVP: second camera, first-person, collective belief, multiple species, documents/libraries, culture traditions as systems (culture = chronicle tallies only), Steward, Ledger auto-promotion, priority matrix, offline catch-up, relationships beyond family + friend/rival, full combat, structural physics beyond cave-in spans, drones, belts.
- Cut entirely: death-farming/mana-for-deletion (prm idea), XP from re-mining placed blocks.
